COMMON WEATHER | A rain wardrobe for the ride and the walk

We are building a three-piece wardrobe for adults who cycle to work and spend weekends on local trails. The same clothes need to work on the bike, at a café table and on foot with a small daypack. The range comprises CW01 Hooded Shell, CW02 Layering Vest and CW03 Overtrousers. Overall silhouette and panel design are open. Our existing R1 zipper pull and webbing buckle are the shared hardware identity. Print "COMMON WEATHER" and "Ride out. Walk on." on the inside neck labels of CW01 and CW02 and the inside waistband label of CW03.

Develop the main garments in size M. CW01 has a fixed hood, a 720 mm front body length, an 800 mm back body length, an 80 mm collar and a 690 mm sleeve measured from shoulder point to cuff, including a 30 mm extension over our 660 mm reference sleeve. It has three zipped pockets: chest storage for a phone measuring 170 × 85 × 12 mm, right-hand storage for gloves packed to 220 × 110 × 40 mm and left-hand storage for a cap packed to 180 × 100 × 30 mm. Place one 60 × 10 mm reflective tab on each outer cuff and one 120 × 10 mm strip across the center back, 100 mm above the back hem. CW02 sits beneath CW01; CW03 has articulated knees and a buckle adjustment at each ankle. Use F01 for the shell and overtrousers: a 125 g/m², 1450 mm-wide, 2.5-layer laminate with a 100% nylon 40-denier plain-weave face and polyurethane membrane. F02 for the vest is a 180 g/m², 1500 mm-wide, 100% polyester brushed tricot. The main color is Moss #465B57, with Clay #B86E50 available for panels and trims.

Audience: our product lead, pattern cutter and sample room will use the work to choose the range and prepare its first samples for adults seeking one wardrobe for commuting and light outdoor use.
