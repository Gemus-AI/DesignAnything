INDEX 38 | Watch macro photography and reflection control

We are commissioning a six-image feature for INDEX 38, our watch editorial series. The subject is one hand-wound wristwatch: a 38 mm stainless steel case excluding the crown, 9 mm thick, with a 46 mm lug-to-lug length and 20 mm strap width. Its polished bezel meets a horizontally brushed case flank; the strap is smooth charcoal leather, #202020, with a brushed steel pin buckle. The three identity images establish the same watch from the front, crown side and buckle side.

The matte ivory dial, #E8E2D6, has 11 single applied steel hour batons, a double baton at 12 and 60 fine black minute ticks. Two polished dauphine hands indicate 10:10, with a slender blue seconds hand pointing at 6. The dial carries "MANUAL" above the 6 o'clock marker. Photograph the series on a matte #D8D3CA sweep with a 5600 K key from camera upper left, elevated 45 degrees, and fill at one quarter of the key's intensity. Use the two black flags in the lighting diagram to define the polished edges. The two macro photographs use 1:1 reproduction, f/8 and 12-frame focus stacks. The editorial page carries "INDEX 38" and the headline "Light reveals the finish".

Audience: watch editors and collectors, for selecting feature photography and examining the watch's dial construction, steel finishes and strap hardware.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the watch specifications, lighting values and editorial wording. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md records the six deliverables and their proportions, formats and stages. watch-geometry-and-lighting.png supplies the measured watch envelope, macro settings and lighting arrangement. watch-front-identity.png establishes the dial, hands, markers and frontal case shape. watch-side-identity.png establishes the crown, bezel profile, brushed flank and lug shape. watch-buckle-identity.png establishes the leather strap and pin buckle. empty-still-life-table.png provides the empty tabletop, sweep and light atmosphere.

Deliverables:
1. Two whole-watch hero images from one frontal exposure, one square crop and one portrait crop, each showing the complete case and both lug pairs
2. One dial macro photograph showing the double 12 o'clock baton, central hand assembly and adjacent minute ticks
3. One material macro photograph showing the crown, polished bezel, brushed case flank and upper crown-side lug
4. One tabletop scene photograph showing the complete watch, its loosely curved leather strap and visible pin buckle
5. One A4 editorial page combining the square hero, dial macro and material macro in that reading order, with the series name, headline and three captions

Acceptance:
