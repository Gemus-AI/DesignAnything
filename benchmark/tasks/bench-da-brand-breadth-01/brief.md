AFTER HOURS WASH | A neighbourhood laundry for the evening shift

We are opening a single self-service laundry for urban renters doing their washing after work. Build an identity that helps people spot the shop from the street, choose a machine on their first visit and settle into the waiting area. Print the name "AFTER HOURS WASH" on all six finished pieces, using one resolved wordmark; capitalisation and line breaks may vary. Print the descriptor "Self-service laundry" on the fascia, entrance window and price card. The optional line is "A lighter load after dark." The logo, colours and typography are open for development.

We open daily from 17:00–02:00 next day, with last wash start at 00:30 and last dry start at 01:20, both after midnight in that evening's session. Group A, machines W1 and W2, washes up to 8 kg for 40 minutes at CNY 24 per cycle. Group B, W3 and W4, washes up to 12 kg for 40 minutes at CNY 32 per cycle. Group D, D1, D2, D3 and D4, dries up to 12 kg for 30 minutes at CNY 16 per cycle. Prices include tax; wash cycles include automatic detergent, and drying is purchased separately. The fascia artwork surface is 4000 mm wide by 600 mm high; the entrance-window surface is 900 mm wide by 1800 mm high. The attached machine image anchors the recognisable front-loading equipment form wherever equipment is illustrated.

Audience: the shop owner and signage supplier, choosing the finished identity and preparing the exterior graphics, interior boards and customer price card for opening.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the operating hours, service records, customer journey, waiting etiquette and artwork dimensions. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six deliverables with their proportions, formats and stages. laundry-surfaces.png gives the two fixed exterior artwork surfaces and their dimensions. front-loading-machine.png supplies the equipment identity through its circular door, visible drum and rectangular cabinet. evening-materials.png provides atmosphere through folded textiles, warm light and restrained material colours.
