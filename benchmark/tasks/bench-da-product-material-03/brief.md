VELL | An articulated collectible from a flat character

We are bringing our quiet seedling wanderer into an adult collector's hands as one complete articulated toy. Develop the supplied front illustration into rounded solid volumes with a neutral upright height of 160 mm, measured from the common sole plane to the highest leaf tip. Preserve the broad cream head, two green leaf tips, navy oval eyes and tiny curved smile, exactly three coral dots on the character's left cheek, teal pear-shaped torso and yellow front diamond. The short cream arms end in integrated mitten hands; the short navy legs end in broad navy feet. Resolve the depth and back as continuous extensions of these colour regions. Small proportion adjustments may accommodate articulation while retaining the broad-head, pear-body silhouette.

The body has exactly seven articulated connections: JN at the neck, JSR and JSL at the shoulders, JHR and JHL at the hips, and JAR and JAL at the ankles. Develop their mechanisms, axis locations and part cuts around the four states P0 neutral, P1 wave, P2 sit and P3 welcome. Use opaque rigid polymer or resin for the head and torso, with named proposed materials for the limb shells, bearing parts and feet, and a recorded matte/satin finish allocation. All eight body units remain attached in the posed views, scene and video. Print the name "VELL" and descriptor "Quiet seedling wanderer" on the translation board, display scene caption and presentation cover.

Audience: our character owner, product designer and model-making workshop, choosing the sculpt and articulation scheme for adult collectors who value character, touch, posing and shelf display.
