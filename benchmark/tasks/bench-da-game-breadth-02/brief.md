SKYWAKE | Sail-glider racing above a sea of clouds

We are building SKYWAKE for racing players who enjoy fast movement and choosing their line through open space. Design the visual world of its first cloud-island circuit, connecting sail-gliders, riders, floating checkpoints and the race interface. The art direction is fixed: painted cloud masses, sharply cut sail silhouettes, ivory fabric, navy enamel and exposed copper fittings. Use #132C46 for navy and #F3B544 for active race signals. Sunlight comes from world northwest at an elevation of 35 degrees. Print "SKYWAKE" and "Ride the high line" on the presentation cover and the video's closing frame.

The circuit occupies 800 m by 600 m and runs clockwise through four gates over two laps, with six racers. Dawn Shelf covers the southern straight; Needle Reach covers the eastern climb through the islands. Gate centres, obstacles, landmarks and two race snapshots are fixed in 03-race-records.en.md. Build the glider inside a 4200 mm length, 6000 mm sail span and 3000 mm overall height; collision uses a rounded hull volume measuring 4200 mm by 1800 mm by 1600 mm. The existing event emblem and checkpoint buoy carry through the work. Upcoming gates use an ivory hollow circle, the active gate uses an amber double chevron, and cleared gates use a navy panel with a white check. The interface uses a 1920 by 1080 logical canvas with horizontal insets of 96 units and vertical insets of 54 units. Default text is at least 26 logical units high. Both input versions carry "Trim sail" and "Reset to last gate" in matching positions.

Audience: the game director, environment artists, vehicle artists and interface team, for approval of the first circuit's visual language and production asset brief.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the fixed art direction, dimensions, identity features and interface requirements. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists each deliverable's proportion, format and stage. 03-race-records.en.md contains the circuit coordinates, race rules, asset records, camera settings and interface snapshots. course-topology.png supplies the measured circuit boundary, gate spacing and spatial anchors. glider-envelope.png supplies the vehicle envelope and collision geometry. race-interface-safe-area.png supplies the logical canvas, safe area and control anchors. event-emblem-and-buoy.png supplies the existing emblem and buoy identity. cloud-sea-sailing-reference.png supplies cloud light, sail tension and material atmosphere.
