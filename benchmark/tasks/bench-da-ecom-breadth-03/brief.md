RASTER FOLD | A type family storefront built around useful specimens

We run an independent type studio and are preparing the direct-store launch of RASTER FOLD, a modular display family for posters, websites and compact brand identities. Its existing letterforms are fixed by the attached glyph masters: Regular 400 and Bold 700 share 47 characters, a 5 by 7 grid and a 24-unit advance. Regular uses 2-unit square modules; Bold uses 3-unit square modules on the same 4-unit pitch. Build the product story around the difference those two weights make in use. Set specimen lettering from those masters; supporting store copy can use a separate readable typeface.

The four purchase options are Desktop, RF-D, USD 60 for 2 named users; Web, RF-W, USD 90 for 1 domain and 50,000 pageviews per calendar month; App, RF-A, USD 150 for 1 app and 10,000 monthly active users; and Ads, RF-AD, USD 120 for 1 campaign and 1,000,000 total impressions. Each is a one-time payment covering both weights, with ongoing use within its stated scope. Print "RASTER FOLD" and "Two weights. One grid." on the main hero, vertical promotion and complete product page. Label each application specimen "APPLICATION STUDY". The store has a square hero, a vertical promotion and landscape detail panels; the attached frame diagram fixes their safe areas. The print application is an A3 poster.

Audience: independent designers and small teams choosing a type family and the appropriate licence for a print, web or brand project.
