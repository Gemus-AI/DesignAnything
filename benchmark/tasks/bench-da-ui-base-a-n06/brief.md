WARDROBE DAY | A personal wardrobe and everyday outfit planner

We are commissioning WARDROBE DAY for young adults who want to use the clothes they already own and decide what to wear before the day begins. The app connects a seven-item wardrobe, saved outfits and everyday occasions. Garment images and fields are supplied; adding and editing garments are manual actions. Each footwear pair counts as one item. At Monday 14 September 2026, 08:00, UTC+08:00, five items are available; Cream knit sweater is in laundry until 18:00 that day, and Indigo straight jeans is loaned until Tuesday 15 September at 12:00. Print "WARDROBE DAY", "7 items" and "5 available" on the main wardrobe screen. Use "Save outfit" for saving an outfit and "Confirm plan" for committing it to an occasion. Availability labels are "Available", "In laundry" and "Loaned". Navigation and visual direction are open.

The central task is planning Rain walk for Coffee catch-up. Its unsaved draft contains Navy Oxford shirt, Charcoal straight trousers, White low-top sneakers and Olive hooded jacket. The initial 09:30–10:30 time overlaps Studio review at 09:00–10:00; the shirt and trousers are the two reserved items. The user chooses the permitted 11:00–12:00 alternative, saves Rain walk and confirms Coffee catch-up. A complete outfit has one top, one bottom and one footwear record, with optional outerwear. Availability is assessed over the occasion's interval. The planning outlook for 14 September is 18°C and light rain; label it "Planning outlook" beside the dismissible outer-layer suggestion, with distinct suggested, awaiting-confirmation and applied states. Use platform-neutral phone screens at a logical 402 × 874 pt, with top and bottom safe areas of 62 pt and 34 pt. Body text contrast is at least 4.5:1; large text and meaningful icons are at least 3:1. Availability and feedback combine colour with text and symbols.
