NORTH SPAN | Sport and everyday passage beneath the viaduct

We are turning the space beneath our neighbourhood viaduct into a public sports ground that also carries everyday walking and cycling. The direction is fixed: exposed concrete columns, clearly bounded activity bays, a continuous southern through-route and planted seating along the northern edge. The rectangular site measures 72 m east–west by 24 m north–south, totalling 1,728 m². Six existing columns stand on a 24 m longitudinal grid and a 12 m transverse grid. Each column has a 4 m × 4 m protection square. The existing ground rises northward at 1%; the minimum clearance beneath the bridge is 6,000 mm.

Organise the site into four fixed bands: Shared Way, 288 m²; Service Strip, 108 m²; Activity Floor, 1,116 m²; and Garden Edge, 216 m². The Shared Way is a continuous 4 m-wide connection between the west and east entrances. The Service Strip contains the existing drainage channel. The Activity Floor contains a 16 m × 12 m basketball practice enclosure, two adjoining 6 m × 10 m table-tennis envelopes and one 6 m × 10 m fitness envelope. Their coordinates are fixed in the site records. Spectator seating and approaches occupy the remaining space around these envelopes. Garden Edge contains six planted beds, each 6 m × 2 m, with a combined palette of 72 Dryopteris filix-mas, 96 Carex pendula and 120 Geranium macrorrhizum. Public access runs from 06:00 to 22:00; sports use runs from 08:00 to 21:00. Lighting serves passage until 22:00. All new fixtures are freestanding and at most 3,000 mm high.

Print the site name as "NORTH SPAN". Use "Shared Way" and "Activity Floor" as the corresponding wayfinding labels. Print all four zone names and all three botanical names on the masterplan and ground and planting sheet, using the spelling in the site records.

Audience: pedestrians and cyclists crossing the neighbourhood, nearby residents using the sports facilities, and the public-space team deciding the layout and operating arrangements.
