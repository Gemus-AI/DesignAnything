LAST LIGHT | One cinema, three ages, a final screening

We are commissioning the narrative design and animated preview for a 15-second short about Oriel Cinema's last screening. Mara returns aged 38 in 2026; the feel of a ticket brings back her first visit aged 8 in 1996 and an evening aged 23 in 2011, just before she left the city. All three visits are on 18 September, for The Long Way Home at 19:30, in seat E6. The ending is a small personal gesture: she leaves her ticket on the empty seat. Print "LAST LIGHT" over the final shot and retain the spoken line "Save me a seat." exactly.

The narrative structure is fixed: 12 shots across four beats, running 2026 → 1996 → 2011 → 2026, with beat boundaries at 0, 3, 7, 11 and 15 seconds. Visual style and individual camera choices are open. Mara's oval face, prominent ears and mole on her right cheek connect all three ages. The north screen, rear projection window and foyer mosaic reading "ORIEL" remain in the same positions. Ticket dimensions are 70 mm × 40 mm; their layout stays constant, with year-specific ink colors #24364B, #8D3D36 and #2D5846 respectively. Develop the specification first, then carry its timing, identity and spatial decisions through the images and preview.

Audience: adult viewers interested in personal growth and urban cultural memory, with the director and animation producer using this package to approve the narrative and begin production planning.
