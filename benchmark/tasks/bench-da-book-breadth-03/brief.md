THE SECOND BELL | Reading structure and typography for a suspense novel in two voices

Design the cover and reading structure for THE SECOND BELL, a literary suspense novel following Mara and Ivo during one cinema's closing night. The first reading follows M1 → I1 → M2 → I2 → M3 → I3, pairing their accounts at 22:10, 22:26 and 22:40. After I3, readers can follow Mara through M1 → M2 → M3 or Ivo through I1 → I2 → I3. The supplied manuscript fixes the events and headings; chapter presentation, typography and abstract cover imagery are open. Introduce the resolution at M3, then develop it through I3 and the reread map.

The edition is a 120 × 160 mm perfect-bound paperback with 176 interior pages on 80 gsm Munken Print Cream 15, an uncoated cream paper with a sheet thickness of 0.12 mm. Its 88 leaves give a spine width of 88 × 0.12 = 10.56 mm. The cover wrap measures 250.56 × 160 mm before the 3 mm outer bleed. The 22 supplied positions are the designed sample positions within this edition: front cover 1; entry 2–3; Mara opener 4–5; Ivo opener 6–7; six story spreads 8–19; reread map 20–21; back cover 22. Interior sample folios retain these numbers. Print "THE SECOND BELL" on the front and spine, with spine lettering running from top to bottom; the optional front subtitle is "A story in two voices". The complete back-cover copy is "One cinema. Two voices. One last night. A locked door, an empty case and a familiar sound leave Mara and Ivo listening for different answers."

Audience: adult literary readers who reconstruct events from different characters' clues, with the editorial and design teams using the work to approve the reading experience and book design.
