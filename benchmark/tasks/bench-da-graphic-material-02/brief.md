LETTER / FORM | Looking closely at a 1785 type specimen

We are commissioning the exhibition graphics for LETTER / FORM at Marlow Print Room, running 6–28 November 2027, Tuesday–Sunday, 11:00–18:00, with Free entry. Print the exhibition name as "LETTER / FORM" and the subtitle as "Looking closely at a 1785 type specimen". The exhibition invites visitors to examine four letter shapes from A specimen of printing types, published in 1785: uppercase C and lowercase g from the Five-Line Pica group in Archive A, scan 13, and uppercase Greek theta and omega from the third row of the Two-Line Pica group in Archive B, scan 17. These supplied letterforms are the visual starting point. Use direct scan crops or faithful tracings, preserving complete contours, openings, connections and orientation through proportional enlargement, isolation or recolouring.

The six finished pieces will serve three reading situations: an A1 portrait poster viewed from 3 m, specimen labels viewed from 50 cm, and a take-away foldout accompanied by a portrait foyer screen. Each label measures 150 × 100 mm. The foldout opens to 297 × 210 mm and closes to 99 × 210 mm, with three equal concertina panels and folds at 99 mm and 198 mm. All printed pieces have 3 mm bleed and a minimum 5 mm text inset from trim; foldout text also sits at least 5 mm from folds. The screen has a 5% text inset on every edge. Source attribution belongs in the reading hierarchy: the short credit is "Source: A specimen of printing types, 1785." and the full credit is "William Caslon (1754–1833), A specimen of printing types, 1785. University of California Libraries / Internet Archive. Public-domain scan."

Audience: design exhibition visitors, including readers encountering historical type specimens for the first time, deciding to enter the exhibition and choosing which letter shapes to examine more closely.
