PIP-01 FIELD SCOUT | Collectible packaging for display and storage

Our collectors keep the box. Develop one reusable package for PIP-01 FIELD SCOUT that becomes its display setting and gives each accessory a permanent storage position. One set contains three physical pieces: one F01 Field Scout figure, one A01 Signal paddle and one A02 Sample canister. The five identity images fix their forms and matte colors, including F01's attached mast and backpack. F01 measures 76 W × 58 D × 112 H mm overall; A01 measures 28 W × 50 H × 8 mm thick; A02 measures 26 mm in diameter × 32 H mm. The maximum closed external package is 180 W × 160 H × 100 D mm. Develop one structure through a specification first, then its applications.

F01 needs at least 82 W × 64 D × 115 H mm clear accommodation above its sole support plane. A01 needs a separate recess with 54 × 32 mm clear plan space and 10 mm clear depth; A02 needs a separate upright recess with 28 mm clear diameter and 34 mm clear height. Both accessories need retrieval access. Choose the materials, nominal thicknesses, actual box dimensions and repeatable closure. The same box and insert must support closed storage, open display with all three pieces within the package system, and repacking. Print "PIP-01 FIELD SCOUT" above "Collectible display set" in the front hierarchy. The back copy is "Set includes", followed by "1 × Field Scout figure (F01)", "1 × Signal paddle (A01)", "1 × Sample canister (A02)", and "Decorative collectible. No electronic functions." Across the two sides, print the set name, F01, A01 and A02, plus concise opening and repacking instructions.

Audience: collectors who retain packaging, and our product and packaging teams deciding how the set will be displayed, stored and prepared for sampling.
