LUMA | AI glasses and a charging case for everyday transitions

We are developing LUMA for adults who want to capture a moment during an urban pause and move comfortably between work and leisure. The system comprises one complete pair of AI glasses and one matching charging/storage case. Connected assistance uses an intentionally captured image and a short, user-initiated spoken question through a paired phone. The glasses themselves are the main design task: two clear non-display lenses, a complete frame, folding temples, integrated electronics, comfortable contact surfaces and reachable physical controls. Print "LUMA" on the glasses and case.

The glasses mass target is at most 42 g. Choose a front width of 140–150 mm and a hinge-axis-to-tip centreline length of 135–150 mm for each temple; the largest temple section is at most 18 mm high and 12 mm thick. Folded glasses fit within 150 × 60 × 48 mm; the closed case fits within 170 × 85 × 65 mm, with at least 2 mm cavity clearance on each opposing side of the chosen folded envelope. Use the eleven recorded electronic modules, with 1 mm between envelopes and 1 mm enclosure outside them except at functional openings. The right-temple underside carries two flush pads of diameter 2 mm, spaced 6 mm centre to centre, both within 60 mm of the hinge; P+ is nearer the hinge. Matching case tips have diameter 1.2 mm. Use satin TR90 for the frame and temple shells, anodised aluminium hinges and silicone nose and ear contact surfaces. Develop the colour palette. Make B01 accessible for user replacement and print "User-replaceable B01" beside its removal detail.

The three glasses states are READY, CAPTURE and PRIVACY. READY exposes the camera with its indicator off; CAPTURE exposes the camera with a steady white front indicator; PRIVACY closes an opaque slider over the camera and disables camera and microphone capture. Give the slider a visible position mark. Folded glasses dock with the slider closed; the case indicator is steady amber during charging and off during stored/idle. Its indicator remains visible with the lid closed. Label these states on their corresponding design boards.

Audience: the product owner, industrial design team and engineering team, choosing the complete eyewear concept and preparing its first development brief.
