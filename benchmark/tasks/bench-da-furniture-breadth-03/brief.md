WAYKEEP | Modular equipment lockers for shared mobility stations

We operate shared mobility stations and need a freestanding locker system for helmets and personal bags beneath our station canopies. The system uses two column types on a 500 mm pitch, each 600 mm deep and 1800 mm high: S4 contains four small compartments and L2 contains two large compartments. Minimum clear interiors, stated as width × depth × height, are 420 × 500 × 360 mm for small compartments and 420 × 500 × 760 mm for large compartments. Our helmet envelope is 330 × 260 × 250 mm; our backpack envelope is 320 × 220 × 650 mm. Develop the cabinet, compartment interiors and shared operating area around our existing D1 door family: 460 × 400 mm and 460 × 800 mm leaves with the same black recessed paddle and two exposed hinges.

Compact, Standard and Expanded stations have respective footprints of 1500 × 2700 mm, 2500 × 2700 mm and 3500 × 2700 mm. They take one, two and three columns of each type, respectively. Reserve a 1200 mm public aisle at the front and a 900 mm maintenance aisle at the rear, measured with doors and panels closed. Cabinets and doors use 1.5 mm galvanized steel with powder coating #243C46; removable trays use 2 mm 304 stainless steel. Each column joint uses four M8 bolts operated with a 6 mm hex key, and each column base has four M10 floor anchors. The largest dismantled part must fit within 1800 × 600 × 100 mm. Print "WAYKEEP" and "Store your gear. Continue your journey." on every station header. Identify compartment states as "Available", "Occupied" and "Service", each paired with a distinct symbol.

Audience: station users, operations staff and our furniture supplier, supporting the range decision, daily operating plan and fabrication brief.
