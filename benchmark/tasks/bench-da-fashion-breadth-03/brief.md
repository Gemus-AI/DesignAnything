PHASE CHOIR | Digital fashion for a three-part virtual performance

We are producing an independent online music performance called PHASE CHOIR. Develop three digital looks for our performer Aster: PC-01, PC-02 and PC-03. Start with three competing collection directions, then select one for the whole wardrobe. Aster has a 1800 mm neutral standing height, a 225 mm head height, 440 mm between shoulder joints and 280 mm between hip joints. Upper arms measure 320 mm, forearms 280 mm, thighs 450 mm and shins 450 mm. Preserve the character's graphite skin, ivory cropped hair, amber eyes and circular amber temple inserts throughout the dressed views.

Each look has an opaque M01 core covering the torso and pelvis, with two translucent M02 outer panels attached at the shoulder joints. Garment shapes are open for exploration. M01 uses #202532; M02 uses #A8E6E1. The performance moves through Still, Walk and Leap: M02 opacity is 0.35, 0.35 and 0.55 respectively, with peak outward displacement from the neutral panel surface of 0 mm, 80 mm and 180 mm. Shoulder attachments stay fixed as the panels move. Our textile references are polyester interlock and polyester organza; their recorded properties guide digital surface and drape, while virtual joins define construction. Print "PHASE CHOIR" on each look's upper-chest core panel, and use "LIVE IN THREE STATES" on the performance scene, video end frame and presentation cover. The stage has a 6000 mm diameter circular platform, a charcoal background and amber side lighting.

Audience: our digital content director, character artist and animation team, choosing the wardrobe direction and preparing the performance sequence.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the character measurements, textile reference properties, digital material settings, movement records and stage dimensions. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists every deliverable with its proportion, format and stage. avatar-rig-and-poses.png supplies the fixed character proportions, joint geometry and three movement poses. aster-character.png supplies Aster's existing face, hair, skin and temple details. empty-stage.png supplies the performance atmosphere, lighting and palette.
