COMMONHOUR | One rental-home table for work, dinner and moving day

We are commissioning COMMONHOUR, an expandable table with internal storage for a renting household of two adults, A and B. A works from 09:00 to 17:00; B returns at 18:30, and they dine together from 19:00 to 20:00. The table has three states: W, one active workplace; D, two simultaneous dining places; and S, the assembled table stored between uses. W needs a clear surface of at least 1000 × 600 mm, D at least 1200 × 800 mm, both at a height of 720–760 mm. D contains two separate 600 × 400 mm place-setting areas. The complete S envelope, including handles and the stored work kit, is at most 1100 × 600 × 850 mm. The expansion method and silhouette are open. Develop the states from the same identifiable assemblies, with a location for every part throughout conversion.

The room is 3600 × 3000 × 2600 mm. Its entrance-side circulation strip is 900 mm deep; the cabinet, chair parking, door swing and protected window band are fixed in the attached room records. Each active chair needs a 600 × 900 mm floor-use envelope. The household keeps chairs CH-A and CH-B and six work items: laptop L, keyboard K, mouse M, notebook N, folder F and cable box C. In W, L/K/M/N are on the surface and F/C are inside; in D and S, all six are concealed inside the table. Use 18 mm birch plywood with clear water-based lacquer for the tabletop and storage panels, and powder-coated steel tube for the supports. Relocation connections use M6 bolts with captive nuts, operated with a 4 mm hex key; select and record their quantity. Each separated furniture module fits within 1300 × 850 × 250 mm in its packing orientation. The moving route passes through an 800 × 2050 mm doorway, a 900 × 900 mm turning square and a 900 mm-wide corridor. Print "COMMONHOUR" in every image title and on every document cover, followed by the relevant role or state. Print "Work away, dinner ready" on the conversion and storage board.

Audience: the furniture studio and its workshop partner, choosing a table for renters who share limited space between home working and dining and need to take the same furniture to their next home.
