RIVERTHREAD OPERA | A touring identity for The Borrowed Lantern

We are taking The Borrowed Lantern to Alder Town Hall on 27 February 2027 and Bracken Arts House on 6 March 2027. We need a contemporary touring identity that welcomes people encountering our theatre for the first time. Print the company name through the supplied complete mark, "RIVERTHREAD OPERA", and use the audience introduction "Story, song and percussion.". The performance is in English and lasts 90 minutes, including one 10-minute interval. Admission is general admission with unreserved seating. Alder Town Hall opens at 18:30 for a 19:00 performance; Bracken Arts House opens at 14:30 for a 15:00 performance.

The direction starts with our existing assets. Preserve the complete burgundy wordmark and ribbon emblem, nominal colour #8A2534, with their lettering, arrangement and proportions intact. The costume pattern supplies gold upright hollow diamonds with central dots, pale wave bands and a deep blue field. Cropping, repetition and uniform scaling are its permitted transformations; preserve source colours, orientation and internal geometry. Every image deliverable must carry the complete mark and a pattern fragment containing at least one complete upright diamond with its central dot and one recognisable portion of the pale wave band. Develop materials for both stops, with the programme cover and spread prepared for the Alder Town Hall opening.

Audience: first-time opera audiences choosing a performance and preparing for their visit, and our touring and venue teams approving the public materials.
