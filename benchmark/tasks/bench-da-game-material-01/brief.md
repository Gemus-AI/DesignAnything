COPPER HOP | A portrait mobile level for an existing robot hero

We are commissioning one finished side-scrolling level for COPPER HOP, played in portrait orientation during short journeys. Our existing robot, Pip, travels from Dock Steps to Relay Gate, collecting three triangular sparks to power the exit. Build the scene as a disused rooftop waterworks: pale plaster platforms, copper pipes and distant storage tanks. The playable envelope is 20 m long and 4 m high. Pip moves automatically to the right and waits at the unpowered gate; the player's actions are jumping and activating the relay.

Pip is 1 m tall, with an ochre body #C88A32, a navy face #203342, one horizontal ivory eye slit, two teal boots and one ivory backpack. Preserve these features and the attached proportions. Maximum jump rise is 0.6 m and maximum horizontal crossing is 1.5 m. The existing relay is 1.2 m high and 0.6 m wide. Screens use a 402 × 874 pt logical canvas, with safe insets of 62 pt above and 34 pt below. Print "COPPER HOP" on every state screen; use "Jump" and "Activate" for the two controls.

Audience: short-session mobile players, with the art and level teams using the work to approve the route, asset treatment and three playable states.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the character, movement, interface and state records. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists every deliverable's proportion, format and stage. screen-safe-area.png supplies the portrait canvas and safe boundaries. thumb-controls.png supplies the control positions and touch dimensions. pip-model.png supplies Pip's existing views, proportions and jump envelope. relay-model.png supplies the existing relay's geometry and two states. commuter-reference.png supplies the one-handed play context.

Deliverables:
1. One finished level overview showing the 20 m × 4 m playable envelope, Dock Steps, Relay Gate, three numbered spark positions and dimensions for every required jump
2. One finished character and relay sheet with front, side and rear views of each, Pip's idle and jumping poses, the relay's Dormant and Powered states, and close-up and 32 pt-high gameplay-scale depictions of both
3. One finished environment asset sheet containing three platform modules, two copper-pipe foreground pieces, one distant-tank backdrop and the triangular spark
4. Three paired state compositions, each containing a touch screen and its controller counterpart: Entry at Dock Steps with 0/3, Relay Waiting at Relay Gate with 2/3, and Relay Restored at Relay Gate with 3/3

Acceptance:
- The level overview dimensions every required crossing at 1.5 m or less and every upward jump at 0.6 m or less; all three sparks are reachable before Relay Gate, with the third reachable from the waiting position.
- The character and relay sheet and all three state compositions preserve Pip's five identity features and 1 m height relative to the 1.2 m relay; the sheet supplies all six object views, four named poses or states, and both display scales.
- The environment sheet supplies all seven listed assets; the overview and state compositions reuse their forms, separate platform edges from adjacent scenery by at least 3:1 contrast, and leave the playable route and sparks visible through foreground placement.
- Each state composition contains two 402 × 874 pt logical screens. Interface content stays between y=62 and y=840; Jump and Activate occupy 56 × 56 pt targets centred at (346, 766) and (258, 766). Controller counterparts use A for Jump and X for Activate at those same positions.
- All six screens print COPPER HOP, Jump and Activate, with the location and counter assigned above. Text is at least 18 pt with 4.5:1 contrast; icons and unavailable controls achieve 3:1. Entry and Relay Waiting show a lock on the unavailable Activate control.
- Relay Waiting shows the relay's amber square Dormant indicator; Relay Restored shows its teal circular Powered indicator and an activated check mark on the Activate control. The character and relay sheet repeats both indicator shapes and colours.
