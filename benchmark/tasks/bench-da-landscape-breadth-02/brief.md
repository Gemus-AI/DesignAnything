REEDHAVEN | Coastal wetland birdwatching trail

We are opening a wetland trail for birdwatchers and school groups while retaining the existing patrol boardwalk. The site is a 240 m east–west by 120 m north–south rectangle, covering 28,800 m². Coordinates start at the southwest corner, with north in the positive y direction. Visitor Margin occupies y = 0–40 m and 9,600 m²; Habitat Buffer occupies y = 40–60 m and 4,800 m²; Habitat Core occupies y = 60–120 m and 14,400 m². Public paths, supports, roofs and furniture belong entirely within Visitor Margin. Habitat Buffer and Habitat Core remain reserved for habitat management. The existing weathered timber patrol boardwalk runs across the site at y = 8–11 m, with a 3 m clear width and deck level +3.20 m.

Build the public route from the west entrance at (0,20) to two observation nodes centred at (80,32) and (180,32). Bird Hide has an 8 m by 6 m footprint, roof shade and capacity for 24 people; Rest Deck has a 6 m by 4 m footprint, slatted shade and capacity for 12 people. Public walking surfaces are +3.00 m, with a minimum clear route width of 2.40 m. Ground falls from +2.80 m at y = 20 m to +0.20 m at y = 100 m. Low tide is +0.20 m and high tide is +1.80 m, all relative to the same site datum. Retain the reed patches and their clear viewing corridors at x = 80 m and x = 180 m. The plant schedule comprises Juncus maritimus, Puccinellia maritima and retained Phragmites australis. Print "REEDHAVEN" on the masterplan, both signs, the materials and planting schedule, and the presentation; print "Watch quietly. Stay on the path." on both signs.

Audience: visiting birdwatchers, school groups and patrol staff, with the reserve team using the work to agree the route, observation facilities and daily access arrangements.
