NOOK COMMUNITY KITCHEN | Shared kitchen action signs

Nook Community Kitchen is opening a shared cooking room for adults with different first languages and levels of cooking experience. We need six finished artworks that make each person's next action and responsibility easy to recognise. The room has preparation benches, a dedicated handwashing basin, a separate utensil sink and drying rack, electric hobs and three open storage bays. Each participant resets their own workstation; the session host opens and closes the room.

The main sequence is WASH HANDS, PREPARE, COOK, SWITCH OFF, CLEAN, RETURN. Handwashing and utensil washing need distinct pictures. Switching off and leaving a hob to cool are separate actions. Facing the storage counter, the bays run BOARDS, COLANDERS, PANS from left to right. The graphic language, typography and colours are open. Print "Nook Community Kitchen" on all six artworks and "Every user resets their own station." on the overview. Use short labels beside recognisable actions and objects, with arrows or numbering carrying the sequence.

Audience: adults sharing the kitchen use the signs to choose their next action, and the session host uses the departure card to decide whether the room is ready to lock.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the shared operating facts, artwork dimensions and reading conditions. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six delivery files with their proportions, formats and stages. kitchen-facts.en.md contains the equipment, responsibilities, local sequences and return rules. required-copy.en.md contains the exact wording assigned to each artwork. kitchen-installation.png locates each prompt relative to its equipment and gives its mounting dimensions. kitchen-equipment.png supplies the equipment forms that the action drawings refer to. kitchen-in-use.png supplies the context of adults sharing counters and moving between stations.

Deliverables:
1. One overview wall guide, 400 × 600 mm, headed SHARED KITCHEN, with six numbered action illustrations in the order WASH HANDS, PREPARE, COOK, SWITCH OFF, CLEAN, RETURN and the responsibility line
2. One preparation sign, 300 × 300 mm, headed BEFORE YOU PREPARE, with WASH HANDS followed by PREPARE and the instruction Use the handwashing basin.
3. One hob reset sign, 300 × 300 mm, headed HOB HANDOVER, addressing HOB USER, with SWITCH OFF followed by LEAVE TO COOL and the instruction Do not wipe a hot hob.
4. One wash-and-return strip, 480 × 270 mm, headed WASH AND RETURN, with four utensil illustrations in the order WASH, RINSE, DRY, RETURN
5. One storage destination sign, 480 × 270 mm, headed RETURN DRY EQUIPMENT, with BOARDS, COLANDERS, PANS and their equipment graphics aligned left to right above the three bays
6. One departure check card, 100 × 150 mm, headed BEFORE LOCKING UP, addressing SESSION HOST, with four empty tick boxes beside Hobs switched off; Work surfaces clear; Utensils clean and dry; Equipment in the right bays
