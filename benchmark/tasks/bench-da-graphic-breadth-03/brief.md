BETWEEN LINES | Personal correspondence stationery

I am commissioning a correspondence set for my own letters: an unhurried greeting, a specific, modest thank-you, and remembering an ordinary shared day. My signature is Rowan Vale. Start with three different graphic directions, then select one and develop the complete set around it. The graphic theme, palette and typography are open. Print "BETWEEN LINES" and "Rowan Vale" on the selected-direction rules sheet and coordinated-use board. Collection and owner wording on individual masters is optional and belongs outside the protected writing regions; Rowan remains the signature within the supplied demonstration letter.

The greeting and thank-you letters are each 148 × 210 mm, with a continuous 110 × 150 mm writing region; the brief note is 105 × 148 mm, with a 75 × 95 mm writing region. The small envelope has two 162 × 114 mm faces and holds either letter folded once across its height. The long envelope has two 220 × 110 mm faces and holds the note flat. The postcard has two 148 × 105 mm faces; the keepsake has two 105 × 148 mm faces, with a 75 × 95 mm memory region on its back. Coordinates below are x, y, width, height in mm from each face's upper-left corner. Small envelope: recipient (58, 40, 88, 46), stamp (126, 8, 28, 26), back sender (12, 76, 74, 25). Long envelope: recipient (108, 40, 90, 44), stamp (184, 8, 28, 26), back sender (12, 73, 78, 25). Postcard back: message (8, 10, 62, 85), recipient (80, 43, 60, 45), stamp (112, 8, 28, 26). Keep the writing regions light and visually quiet, with optional fine writing guides; reserve address and stamp regions as blank paper. Prepare reusable, unfilled masters, with 3 mm bleed outside trim and a minimum 5 mm safety margin for printed wording. Envelope artwork describes closed front and back faces. Both faces of every pair share their size and orientation.

Audience: adults maintaining relationships through handwritten correspondence, with Rowan Vale choosing the set for personal use and the printer preparing the paper pieces.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the dimensions, protected regions, use cases, messages and address records. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists every deliverable's proportion, format and stage. 03-paper-zones.png maps the fixed face dimensions and protected regions. 04-rowan-handwriting.png records Rowan's handwriting for the filled demonstration. 05-sealing-a-letter.png provides the setting and handling context of personal correspondence.
