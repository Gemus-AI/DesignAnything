CITY AFTER DARK | A permanent exhibition about urban night life

We are turning one gallery at Riverside Nature Centre into a permanent exhibition for residents, families and school groups. The exhibition name is "CITY AFTER DARK"; the entrance must also carry "Nature begins at your doorstep". Our direction is fixed: visitors observe an object, explore how the animal uses its surroundings, then consider an everyday encounter with urban nature. Arrange the three zones in this order: Tree Canopy, Garden Edge, Damp Path. Use deep blue #182C35 and warm white #F3EBDD throughout the interpretation.

The gallery measures 12000 × 8000 mm with a clear height of 3200 mm. Its west entrance and east exit are each 1800 mm wide. Preserve the 1500 mm circulation strips along the west, south and east walls, the exhibition boundary and both benches shown on the survey. Visits last 30 minutes; school groups contain 24 pupils and the operating capacity is 36 visitors. Our six exhibits, E01–E06, comprise three animal specimens, two touchable teaching replicas and one screen. E03, the Red fox skull, is replaceable through staff access while its cabinet stays in position. E02 and E04 belong within their main stations as touchable objects. E06 uses the three habitat pairings and response text in the exhibit records.

Use 2400 mm exhibition walls, 800 mm cabinet and touch-table tops, 1100 mm exhibit-label centres and an 850 mm screen-control centre. Each specimen cabinet uses the 12-part assembly recorded in the fact sheet, with M6 connections operated by a 5 mm hex key and two storage crates per cabinet. Open and Closed are distinct operating states: Open uses 50 lux at specimens, 150 lux at labels and 250 lux at direction signs; Closed uses 5 lux at specimens and 50 lux along the circulation route, with labels and the screen switched off.

Audience: the centre's learning, collections and facilities teams, for the exhibition layout decision, interpretation development and installation brief.
