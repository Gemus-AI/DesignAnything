QUARTO | Four-formula concentrate sampler

We are refreshing the labels and retail four-pack for QUARTO, our range of 60 mL drink concentrates. The existing wordmark and four-quarter device stay together, in ink blue #17307A or reversed to white. The clear PET bottle, white ribbed PET screw cap and tamper band are fixed: body diameter 34 mm, closed height 100 mm and cap height 16 mm. Each 100 x 40 mm label sits in the straight zone starting 20 mm above the base, with a nominal 6 mm rear gap. Develop the typography, formula accents and carton structure around these existing assets. Pack language is limited to product identities, supplied ingredient descriptions, preparation and materials information.

The sampler contains F1 FOCUS, F2 HYDRATE, F3 CALM and F4 BOOST, one bottle each, upright in that left-to-right order. Their accents are teal #1F8A8A, sea blue #2C6FD1, lavender #7B6BC2 and amber #E08A1E respectively. The paperboard carton has a maximum closed envelope of 160 mm wide x 46 mm deep x 112 mm high; its opening and bottle retention are yours to resolve. Any cut-out remains open. The inserted recycling card is 105 x 74 mm. Print "QUARTO" through the supplied mark and "60 mL concentrate" on every bottle label. Codes and names carry formula recognition throughout the range.

Audience: consumers choosing a small-format concentrate sampler on a convenience-store shelf, with the brand and packaging teams using the work to select the retail treatment.
