MAPLE COURT | Community park for shade and intergenerational activity

We are turning the Toronto site shown in the attached aerial into a neighborhood park for walking, accompanying children, quiet sitting and light activity. The project name is "MAPLE COURT". Work within the existing A–D boundary: approximately 4,882 m², with AB 64.1 m, BC 76.3 m, CD 64.2 m and DA 75.9 m. Preserve the positions of existing trees and retained features recorded in the attached site information. The aerial supplies the site's recognizable surroundings.

Organize four zones: Grove Walk, 1,482 m²; Play Commons, 1,200 m²; Quiet Garden, 1,000 m²; and Open Lawn, 1,200 m². Paths count within these areas. Provide a continuous 1.5 m walking loop with a proposed 1% crossfall, a dense planted grove and an open grass area. The summer layout needs 900 m² of covered walking and sitting surfaces: 720 m² beneath tree canopies and 180 m² beneath shelters, counted as separate footprints. Add 18 trees and the shrub and groundcover layers listed in the fact sheet. Place 120 m² of permeable concrete within Play Commons. Four nodes organize daily use: N1 caregiver seating in Grove Walk, N2 shared activity in Play Commons, N3 quiet seating in Quiet Garden and N4 gentle exercise in Open Lawn. Separate N2 and N3 with a planted strip at least 8 m wide. Print the project name and "Room for every day" on the masterplan and planting schedule; repeat the zone names and botanical names exactly between those two deliverables.

Audience: neighborhood residents and the parks team, deciding the preferred layout and the scope of the next design stage.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md records the site dimensions, area schedule, planting quantities and activity requirements. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the deliverables, proportions, formats and stages. site-overhead.png is the identity source, showing the real historical aerial and surrounding context. site-plan.png is the constraint source for the existing boundary, north, scale and surroundings. source-status.md records the aerial's date, source and reuse terms, the measured design extent and the site assumptions.
