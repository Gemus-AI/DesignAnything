FIELDWEAVE | A textile replacement strap for PineTime

We are commissioning a replacement strap for the existing PineTime shown in watch-front.jpg, for people who want to keep their watch and change how it feels during commuting and light activity. Retain its black rounded rectangular case, narrow reflective bezel edge, rectangular display opening, single side button and original back with its sensor and charging functions. The published case dimensions are 37.5 × 40 × 11 mm. Use the two original mounting positions and spring bars, with their recessed retention; each strap terminal is 20 mm nominal.

The strap is two separate pieces of woven polyester textile, with polyester stitching and stainless steel closure and rigid hardware. Develop one colourway and one closure covering wrist circumferences of 150–200 mm. Weave, colour, edge treatment, steel finish, nominal segment lengths and adjustment travel are yours to resolve. The same pair of pieces and closure serves both fit profiles through closure adjustment: W160 is a 160 mm adult left wrist with a relaxed hand and shirt cuff; W190 is a 190 mm adult left wrist, gently flexed, with casual short sleeves. The closure opens and secures by hand, exposes the length setting and holds the free end. Print the project name "FIELDWEAVE" on boards 1, 2, 5 and 6.

Audience: existing-watch owners and our accessories team, choosing a strap design to take into sampling for daily commuting and light activity.
