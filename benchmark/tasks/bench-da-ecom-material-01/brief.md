DAYSPAN | Fit and size visuals for the Field Overshirt

We are preparing the Field Overshirt product page for DAYSPAN's own online shop. Buyers need to see where the shoulder falls, how the hem sits and what changes when they sit down or reach forward. The garment is fixed: a relaxed, straight-cut overshirt in Clay #8A6754, made from 100% cotton plain weave at 220 g/m², with a matte surface and no stretch. It has a pointed collar, six dark brown front buttons, one left chest patch pocket, single-button cuffs, a straight hem and a straight back yoke.

The range contains three size SKUs: DS-FO-S, Field Overshirt / S; DS-FO-M, Field Overshirt / M; and DS-FO-L, Field Overshirt / L. Each costs USD 68. Finished garment measurements in mm, ordered as flat chest width, back length, shoulder width and sleeve length, are S: 540, 680, 460, 580; M: 570, 700, 480, 590; L: 600, 720, 500, 600. All wearing views use size M on the same generated adult model, with height 172 cm, chest 92 cm, waist 76 cm and hips 98 cm. Use standing, seated and forward-reaching poses with the front fully buttoned and cuffs fastened. Print "AI-generated model" at the lower left of every image containing the model and throughout the video, with letter height at least 5% of the frame's shorter side.
