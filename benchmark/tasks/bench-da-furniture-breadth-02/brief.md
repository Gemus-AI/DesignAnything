NEST STEP | A bedroom system for growing independence

We are developing NEST STEP for families adapting a child's bedroom from shared bedtime routines to independent dressing and tidying. Build one system for two stages: ages 4–6 and ages 7–10. Both stages use the same 1900 × 900 × 120 mm mattress and eight assemblies: one bed frame, one mattress platform, two solid side guards, one bedside cabinet, one caregiver bench and two existing canvas bins. The bins are the starting point for the storage design; retain their sage fabric, rectangular shape and paired loop handles.

The room measures 3200 × 3600 mm, with its door, window and wardrobe fixed as recorded in the attachments. Maintain a 750 mm clear route from the door to the bed's entry. The bed's outer footprint is 1980 × 980 mm. Set the platform top at 180 mm in the younger stage and 300 mm in the older stage. Each side guard is 1100 mm long, starts at the mattress head and reaches 350 mm above the mattress top, leaving an 800 mm entry toward the foot. Both guards remain in use at both stages. The cabinet measures 600 × 320 × 600 mm and the bench 700 × 350 × 420 mm, in width × depth × height. In the younger stage, the bins sit under the bench beside the caregiver; in the older stage, they occupy two cabinet shelves with tops at 120 and 360 mm above the floor.

Use 18 mm birch plywood with clear water-based lacquer for the furniture, exposed edges rounded to R5 mm, and 16 M6 button-head bolts with barrel nuts for the bed assembly, operated with a 4 mm hex key. The cabinet and bench use glued dowel joints. Each dismantled furniture part fits within 2000 × 1000 × 450 mm. Print "NEST STEP" and "Room to grow" on the specification sheet, hero image, video title card and presentation cover.

Audience: families, our product team and the furniture workshop, for choosing the room arrangement and developing the first sample.
