COMMON HOURS | An extension for a working intergenerational community station

Our community station brings children, caregivers and older residents together six days a week. We need a single-storey extension with a Shared Room for 20 people, a Quiet Room for 8 people, a Connecting Lobby and an Activity Store. Give the Quiet Room a view into shared activity and a separate approach: its access route must remain clear when tables, games and group sessions occupy the Shared Room. Print "COMMON HOURS" and "Time together, room to pause" at the completed public entrance.

The site measures 28000 × 20000 mm. The existing brick building measures 12000 × 8000 mm; its floor is ±0.000, roof soffit +3.000 m and roof top +3.400 m. The eastern extension envelope measures 9000 × 12000 mm, with a maximum roof height of +4.200 m. Retain columns C-01 and C-02, walls W-01 to W-04, roof R-01 and the two existing door openings. The new connection uses the 2000 mm opening D-02 in W-04. Existing services operate Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00; the building and southern forecourt remain available throughout those hours. Connection work occupies Sunday, 09:00–17:00, with services resuming Monday at 09:00. Keep existing buff brick exposed; use exposed glulam posts and beams inside the extension, timber rainscreen outside and aluminium window frames. The retained building and these material decisions establish the direction.

Audience: children, caregivers, older residents and station staff, together with the community operator, for agreement on the extension layout and the sequence that keeps daily services running.
