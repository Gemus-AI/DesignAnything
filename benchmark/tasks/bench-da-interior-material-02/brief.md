LOOMFIELD STUDIO | A craft workspace for making, recording and product photography

Our studio occupies a room measuring 6.0 m east–west by 5.0 m north–south, with a clear height of 3.0 m. The origin is the southwest internal corner; x runs east, y north and z upward. Door D1 occupies x = 0.3–1.3 m on the south wall, is 2.1 m high and opens outward on its west hinge. Window WN1 occupies x = 0.6–3.0 m on the north wall, with a sill at z = 1.1 m and head at z = 2.5 m. Column P1 occupies x = 5.6–6.0 m, y = 4.6–5.0 m; service riser R1 occupies x = 5.6–6.0 m, y = 0–0.6 m. Retain this shell, both obstructions and the three socket positions recorded in the fact sheet. The usable floor area is 29.6 m². New furniture, freestanding screens, curtains and surface-mounted fittings form the fit-out.

We make sewn fabric accessories and record their assembly. Retain the timber bench B1, sewing machine E1, manual press E2, photography table T1 and two stools S1–S2. E1 and E2 share B1; their positions and both tables' positions stay the same during making and recording. From 09:00–12:00, one creator uses E1 while an assistant photographs finished products at T1. From 14:00–16:00, the creator records spoken hand-stitching lessons at B1 with E1 switched off, while the assistant retrieves materials. North daylight and speech from the corridor east of the room shape the curtain, microphone and absorption arrangements. Organise Making, Recording, Materials, Finished work and Circulation as five separately measured floor zones. Provide a continuous route at least 900 mm wide, one clear turning circle of 1500 mm diameter, space for 24 material bins and 12 finished-product trays, and an assistant's retrieval route outside the teaching camera's field of view. Print "LOOMFIELD STUDIO" on the teaching backdrop and presentation cover, and "Ready to photograph" on the finished-product storage. Use clear-coated birch plywood WD-01, matte warm-white wall paint PT-01 and wool-felt absorption panels AC-01.
