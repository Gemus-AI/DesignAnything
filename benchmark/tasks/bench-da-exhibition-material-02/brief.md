TIME AT THE TABLE | A neighbourhood exhibition about everyday fermentation

We are commissioning a temporary exhibition for Riverside Community House, open from 7 to 28 November 2026. Visitors should be able to follow a household fermentation story in 5 to 8 minutes: preparing vegetables, adding salt, packing a jar, waiting and serving. The three loan objects are F01 Mixing bowl, 1998, diameter 320 mm and height 140 mm; F02 Wooden tamper, 2004, length 280 mm and diameter 60 mm; and F03 Fermentation jar, 1996, diameter 240 mm and height 360 mm. Their labels and the Mill Lane household notes are supplied in 03-object-and-process-records.en.md. Build the interpretation around these records, with the utensils as the centre of attention. Print "TIME AT THE TABLE" and "Everyday work, shared food" on the introductory graphic.

The room measures 6000 × 4000 mm, with a clear height of 2800 mm. A centred 1200 mm entrance in the south wall faces a centred 1200 mm exit in the north wall; both doors open outward. The connecting 1200 mm-wide passage runs through the room. Two display zones, each 1800 × 2800 mm, sit against the west and east walls, starting 600 mm from the south wall. Each side wall offers 4000 mm of usable length. Arrange the supplied kit within these zones: two 1200 × 600 mm birch plywood tabletops, four folding trestles, two aluminium graphic frames with two replaceable textile graphics, four frame clamps, eight M6 hand knobs and three lift-off acrylic covers. Table surfaces sit 750 mm above the floor; frame tops sit at 1650 mm. Assembly uses hand knobs and clamps. The maximum packed-part envelope is 1200 × 900 × 450 mm, with two storage crates. A fixed bench occupies the southeast corner. Plan for up to 12 visitors at once, including families and wheelchair users.

Audience: the community programme team and exhibition installer, choosing the display arrangement and preparing the room for opening.
