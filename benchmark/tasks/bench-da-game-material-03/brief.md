LOCKREACH | Floodline faction expansion

Our players already know the Reed Union by its split-ring emblem, dark teal #234E52 and ivory #F3EBDD. Floodline expands this faction with two new roles, Ferryman and Surveyor, two terrain tiles and three action cards. Develop the existing visual language: engraved contours, flat color fields and equipment built from timber, rope and brass. The attached emblem and the existing Warden pawn anchor the family resemblance. Print "LOCKREACH" and "Reed Union" on every action card and the shared card back; print "Floodline" on the card back and the presentation cover.

The components fit our current set. Cards are 63 × 88 mm with 3 mm bleed and a 3 mm internal text margin. Their horizontal bands run from the top trim edge: title 0–10 mm, cost and keyword 10–18 mm, illustration 18–60 mm, rules 60–80 mm and footer 80–88 mm. Hex tiles have 40 mm sides. Pawn bases are 25 mm in diameter and 3 mm thick; figures stand 32 mm above the base within a 22 mm maximum width. A white index line on each base points toward the tile's top vertex for Ready and rotates 90 degrees clockwise for Spent. Supply is a filled diamond, Move is a double right chevron and Guard is an outlined square. The action cards are Ferry Run, Cost 1, Move, "Move 1 hex."; Raise Marker, Cost 0, Supply, "Gain 1 Supply."; and Hold Fast, Cost 1, Guard, "Gain 1 Guard.". Reed Landing and Gauge Bank are passable terrain, each occupying one hex.

Audience: existing LOCKREACH players and our product team, deciding whether Floodline reads naturally beside the core set and is ready for component production.
