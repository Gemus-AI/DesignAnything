OPEN FOLD | A shared identity for independent print and making spaces

We run OPEN FOLD, an ongoing cultural-service network in Morrow Ward bringing together four independent spaces with equal standing: Margin Room, an independent bookshop at 18 Alder Lane; Two Tone Press, a print studio at 7 Alder Yard; Loop Bindery, a bookbinding studio at 31 Canal Row; and Form Table, a ceramic workshop at 4 Foundry Walk. We need a shared identity for joint publicity that also leaves each space recognisable. The mark may be a wordmark alone or a wordmark with a symbol. Print OPEN FOLD as the network name, "Independent print + making" as its descriptor and "A member of OPEN FOLD" as the member endorsement.

Member door panels are 240 × 320 mm, with a 15 mm inset for printed content. On these panels, the member name, role and address lead; the network endorsement sits below them. Network posters lead with OPEN FOLD and its descriptor, followed by all four members with their roles at equal credit. Member distinction uses at least one cue from type, layout, pattern, illustration, photography or material texture, with colour as a supporting cue. Event publicity leads with the activity and attendance details. Open Pages is a Small-press display and reading on 22 May 2027, 14:00–17:00, hosted by Margin Room at 18 Alder Lane with Loop Bindery. Ink Together is a Print open studio on 29 May 2027, 11:00–16:00, hosted by Two Tone Press at 7 Alder Yard with Margin Room. Hands at Work is Craft demonstrations on 5 June 2027, 13:00–17:00, hosted by Form Table at 4 Foundry Walk with Loop Bindery. Credit hosts as "Hosted by" and partners as "With". Print "Free entry" and "No booking required" on every event poster and digital card.

Audience: the four space owners and their publicity teams, choosing a shared identity and preparing door signage and event communications for young visitors.
