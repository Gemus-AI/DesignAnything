MODULE / MORNING | Two modules for changing clothes and occasions

We are commissioning a modular brooch system for adults who move between tailored wool and softer everyday layers. The designed objects are two decorative modules, D1 and D2, and their detachable link. Each module works alone on either supplied carrier; the pair also works on either carrier, with one carrier attached to one module and the second module supported through the link. We want an expressive shape language that carries across these changes of position. Print "MODULE / MORNING" and "Two modules, shifting positions" on the final system drawing and on the presentation cover where one is delivered.

The supplied carriers set the rear interface. P serves fabric 1.5–3.0 mm thick: its mounting plate is 24 × 6 × 2 mm and its closed mechanism projects 7 mm behind the mounting face. C serves fabric 0.6–1.2 mm thick: its plate is 28 × 8 × 2 mm and its closed mechanism projects 9 mm behind the mounting face. Both have two M2 threaded holes at 12 mm centre spacing, with centres at (−6, 0) and (+6, 0). Each module needs corresponding 2.4 mm clearance holes, room for the larger C footprint and screwdriver access when removed from clothing. Carrier changes use the supplied screws; the D1–D2 link attaches and detaches by hand. Preserve the covered needle catch, opening clip jaw and soft contact pads, with mounting holes clear of the other module in paired arrangements.

Each module fits within 35 × 35 × 4 mm; every displayed decorative pair fits within 70 × 45 × 4 mm, excluding the carrier. Choose the actual dimensions. The decorative palette is M1, uncoated brushed 316L stainless steel sheet, 1.0 mm thick, and/or M2, through-coloured acrylic sheet, 2.0 mm thick. Allocate the materials and finishes explicitly, soften exposed edges and use integral features for the link. The wearing range moves from a 2.4 mm wool lapel to a 0.8 mm scarf edge, a 1.0 mm shirt placket and a 1.1 mm cardigan pocket edge.

Audience: adults choosing accessories for formal and everyday clothing, with our studio and workshop using the work to select the range and develop its construction.
