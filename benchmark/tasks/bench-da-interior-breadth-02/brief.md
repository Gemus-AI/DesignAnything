COMMON MOVE | A city club for chess, board games and shared viewing

We are fitting out an existing ground-floor room for COMMON MOVE. Its internal envelope is 12 m east–west by 9 m north–south, with a clear height of 3.3 m. Coordinates start at the southwest internal corner, with x east, y north and z above the floor. Entrance D1 occupies x = 1–2.4 m on the south wall; exit D2 occupies y = 1–2.2 m on the east wall. New lightweight partitions are available within it.

The club needs quiet chess in the northwest, teaching and shared viewing to the east, and arrival and game handling near D1. Build a functional and furniture schedule first, then develop these relationships into the interior. Retain six T1 chess tables, three T2 folding game tables and two sage-green S1 game cabinets. Daily sessions seat 12 chess players and 18 board-game players, supported by two hosts. During the weekly event, the 12 chess players stay seated while six beginners learn at one T2 table and 12 spectators watch a wall-mounted display; the other two T2 tables move to a parking trolley. Arrival runs from 18:30 to 19:00, teaching from 19:00 to 19:20, shared viewing from 19:20 to 20:30, and game returns until 20:45. Allow 15 minutes for two hosts to change the social area between layouts. Provide 32 bag compartments and storage for 80 game boxes.

Use a 600 mm occupied-chair allowance beyond each seated table edge, a continuous route at least 1200 mm clear beyond occupied furniture, and 1500 mm turning circles at arrival and a club table. Separate the quiet zone with a full-height lightweight partition and a self-closing door. Carry FL-01 sealed cork flooring, WD-01 clear matte oak and AC-01 wool-felt-faced acoustic panels through the scheme. Print "COMMON MOVE" at arrival and "Quiet Play" at the quiet-zone entrance.
