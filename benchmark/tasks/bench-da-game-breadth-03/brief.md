THE REED COURT | Letters, loyalties and a sealed court secret

We are developing THE REED COURT, an original single-player court correspondence game set in the palace of Avel. The player is Mara Venn, a junior envoy trying to establish who ordered East Gate opened during a royal curfew. Our first playable sequence moves from Arrival into either Confidence or Disclosure. Its emotional centre is a borrowed pendant: Ivo Sere owns it, Mara holds it, and opening it trades his trust for evidence about Tessa Orlan.

The art direction is fixed: ink contours, translucent gouache, parchment #F2E6CC and charcoal #252A30, with candlelight entering from the upper left. House Venn uses rust #8A4934 and a forked reed; House Sere uses blue #315B70 and three vertical reeds; House Orlan uses plum #69445C and a stepped arch. These existing marks identify correspondents alongside their names. The pendant is a silver oval, 36 × 24 × 6 mm, with one top loop and a right-side hinge. Its inner inscription becomes visible in Disclosure. Build around a 1920 × 1080 logical canvas with a 48-unit safe inset, a 960-unit letter column, default text at least 18 logical units high and lines of at most 75 English characters. Reading at 200% uses vertical scrolling. Body text and ordinary controls need 4.5:1 contrast; large text, icons and disabled controls need 3:1. Disabled actions carry a lock icon and the label "Sealed".

Audience: the art director, narrative designer and interface designer, choosing the visual system for a playable opening aimed at solo players who enjoy character relationships and written choices.
