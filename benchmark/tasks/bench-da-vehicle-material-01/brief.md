PARCEL BAY | Delivery enclosure for an existing cargo tricycle

We run short neighbourhood delivery rounds with frequent parcel stops. Design the enclosure, partitions and retrieval opening for our existing Tricycle cargo bike B, version 1.0, documented by A. Pardo and D. Jaeger for Open Hardware Observatory (OHO). Retain its open structural frame, three wheels, steering hardware and rider position. The published overall dimensions are 2131 mm long, 826 mm wide and 1021 mm high; the front-frame base is 900 mm longitudinally by 800 mm transversely. Give the added enclosure purposeful contemporary surfacing and explicit material and finish assignments. Print "PARCEL BAY" and "Neighbourhood delivery" on the enclosure exterior.

Use the attached local datum: x increases towards the handlebar, y runs across the frame and z starts at the cross-member tops. The complete closed enclosure occupies x=40..860, y=170..630 and z=10..430 mm. Four removable clamps connect it to the two existing 20 by 20 mm cross members at C1 (300,200), C2 (300,600), C3 (500,200) and C4 (500,600). Above-base additions occupy x<880 and y=150..650 mm. During O1 opening, every moving or parked enclosure part stays inside x=40..860, y=170..630 and below z=650 mm. The load is three upright, single-layer cartons: P1, 300 by 200 by 180 mm; P2, 240 by 180 by 160 mm; P3, 180 by 140 by 100 mm, each stated as L by W by H. Provide at least 10 mm clear between parcels and around each parcel inside its compartment. R1 removes P2 with P1 and P3 in place; R2 then removes P1 with P3 in its original position. Opening and retrieval take place stationary on level ground.

Audience: our delivery operations lead and fabrication partner, choosing the enclosure arrangement and preparing the prototype brief.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the frame records, installation datum, parcel dimensions and operating cases. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six deliverables with their proportions, formats and stages. frame-overview.png is the identity reference for the complete existing tricycle. frame-plan.png is the identity reference for its front-frame geometry. cross-member.png is the identity reference for retained part 2.11. installation-boundaries.png is the constraint drawing of the installation limits, clamp positions and parcel envelopes. neighbourhood-stop.png is the atmosphere reference for a stationary street-side parcel stop.
