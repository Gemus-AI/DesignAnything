THE ROOM BETWEEN | A surreal fashion editorial built around one coat

We are commissioning a fashion story for our magazine's October 2026 issue. The editorial title is "THE ROOM BETWEEN", with the printed line "One coat, four passages". Our approved subject is Mara Vale, age 32, right-handed, with a centre-parted, jaw-length dark brown bob and a small mole on her left cheek. Her front and profile reference establishes the person throughout the story. Build the surreal setting through constructed scenery, mirrors and in-camera optical effects; the narrative follows Approach, Divide, Reverse and Return, in that order.

Mara wears the same steel-blue coat, colour #5C6670, in every appearance: 100% wool herringbone, 1180 mm shoulder-to-hem length, six matte-black front buttons of 22 mm diameter, one left chest welt pocket, two hip welt pockets and a 300 mm centre-back vent. Styling is black straight-leg trousers and black leather ankle boots, with the coat fastened. Work within the attached 6000 × 8000 mm studio, using its doorframe and mirror. The background is #D8D2C8; the key light is 45 degrees camera-left, centred at 2600 mm, at 5600 K, with a key-to-fill ratio of 2:1. Our magazine pages are 210 × 280 mm, giving a 420 × 280 mm spread with 3 mm outer bleed and a central 24 mm gutter protection band. Faces, buttons and the chest pocket belong outside that band.

Audience: fashion magazine readers encountering the story in print and on mobile, with the picture editor and art director selecting the publication sequence.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the subject, wardrobe, lighting and story records. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists every deliverable's proportion, format and stage. editorial-crop-map.png establishes the page geometry, gutter protection and three photographic crop proportions. studio-plan.png records the studio envelope, backdrop, practical scenery and starting light positions. coat-front-back.png establishes the coat's silhouette, construction and hardware. wool-detail.png establishes the herringbone weave, button finish and pocket stitching. subject-front-profile.png establishes Mara Vale's facial features and haircut. empty-studio-light.png provides an empty-space reference for the background tone and direction of light.
