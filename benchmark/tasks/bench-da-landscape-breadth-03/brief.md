MILL ROW | Rain gardens and a shared street for an old shopping lane

We are renewing MILL ROW so residents can walk, cycle, visit the shops and pause beside planted rain gardens. The project covers an 80 m by 12 m street, totaling 960 m². Retain the four shop entrances, four existing trees, stone curbs and brick shopfronts recorded in the attachments. The direction is fixed: a continuous southern footway, a central shared walking and cycling lane, a rain garden band and a northern shop forecourt. Develop the ground finishes, planting and places to pause within this arrangement.

The four zones are South Walk, 160 m²; Shared Lane, 320 m²; Rain Garden, 160 m²; and Shop Forecourt, 320 m². The street falls east at 0.5%; the lane and forecourt fall toward the garden at 1%. Four level-bottomed beds provide 120 m² of planted storage within the garden band, with 150 mm design ponding and at least 50 mm freeboard at each downstream edge. The drainage brief fixes an 800 m² catchment, 25 mm design rainfall, runoff coefficient 0.90, 18 m³ storage and a 24-hour drawdown target. Maintain a 2 m clear southern footway and four 2 m shop connections across the garden band. Print "MILL ROW" and the four zone names on the masterplan and planting and materials schedule; repeat the plant names Carex elata, Juncus effusus and Iris sibirica in both.

Audience: residents who walk and cycle, shop operators and street maintenance staff, supporting the street layout decision and the detailed design brief.
