STILLFORM | Sculptural rings for everyday stacking

We are developing three sculptural everyday rings, R1, R2 and R3, for adults who use jewellery to express personal style. Each must stand alone on the right middle finger and share a recognisable language of volume, negative space and surface. Explore two contrasting series directions and a third alternative, each containing all three rings, then select one. We are leaving the silhouettes and finish placement open. Print the name "STILLFORM" and the line "Separate forms, shared rhythm" on the single-ring portrait board and presentation cover.

The structural material is 925 silver, cast and then finished with polished, satin or intentionally oxidised surfaces. Each ring has a rigid, closed circular bore of diameter 18.0 mm, a maximum axial extent of 6.0 mm and an outer cylindrical envelope of diameter 28.0 mm. Load-carrying metal sections are at least 1.5 mm thick; skin-contact edges have a minimum radius of 0.5 mm. The fit profile has a resting finger diameter of 17.2 mm and knuckle diameter of 17.6 mm. Positive z runs from the palm-side base crease towards the fingertip: the wearing zone is z = 0–18 mm and all worn ring material sits within z = 2–16 mm, a 14 mm window. S12 places R1 then R2 from palm to fingertip on a relaxed open right hand; S23 places R2 then R3 in the same order on a right hand lightly gripping a plain cylinder of diameter 60 mm. Adjacent rings may touch, with their metal volumes remaining separate.

Audience: our studio and workshop will use the work to choose the series for sampling, with adult everyday wearers as the intended customers.

3. One selected-series rules board naming the chosen direction, shared features, ring differences, finish regions and the axial positions and rotations for S12 and S23
4. Three dimensioned technical sheets, one each for R1, R2 and R3, with front, side and axial views, contact sections and finish boundaries
5. One single-ring portrait board with three labelled views, showing R1, R2 and R3 individually on the right middle finger
6. Two stack wearing boards: S12 on the relaxed open right hand viewed obliquely from its back, and S23 on the right hand lightly gripping the 60 mm cylinder, each with an enlarged contact view and an axial placement diagram
7. One materials and surface sheet mapping the chosen finishes across all three rings, with enlarged skin-contact and ring-to-ring contact details
8. One 10-second wearing video showing S12 on the open right hand and S23 in the light cylinder grip
9. One presentation document of at least 8 pages, reusing the craft note, directions, selection, technical sheets, portraits, stacks, surface details and video stills
