FOLD INTO SCIENCE | A paper transformation opening for a science programme

We are launching FOLD INTO SCIENCE, a general science programme for teenagers and curious adults watching on digital screens. Design the finished storyboard and key artwork for its 18-second opening: six shots in which a paper point becomes a line, opens into a plane, folds into a volume and unfolds to introduce the programme name. The theme words are Point, Line, Plane and Volume. The on-screen copy is "FOLD INTO SCIENCE" and "Science takes shape". The name begins appearing at 12 seconds and is fully readable by 14 seconds; the tagline appears at 15 seconds, followed by a three-second hold of the complete title composition.

The existing material is warm ivory, uncoated paper weighing 160 g/m², with a diagonal crease, visibly fibrous torn edges and a soft shadow cast towards the lower right. Carry these features through the transformations against a background of #263542. The particular silhouettes and transformation mechanisms are yours to develop. The opening has a 16:9 composition and a separately composed 9:16 version. Both use action margins of 3.5% and graphic margins of 5% on every edge. The final title group occupies the attached title area: 20–80% across and 58–82% down the landscape frame; 10–90% across and 55–78% down the portrait frame. Coordinates are measured from the top left.

Audience: the programme producer and motion designer, selecting the finished visual treatment and using its shot timings to build the opening for teenage and general audiences.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md records the programme identity, material, timing and composition facts. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the deliverable proportions, formats and stages. 03-sequence-records.en.md contains the six timed narrative beats and exact screen copy. landscape-safe-areas.png supplies the landscape frame, safety margins and final title area. portrait-safe-areas.png supplies the portrait equivalents. opening-timeline.png locates the transformation beats, title entrance and final hold. ivory-paper-identity.png records the existing paper's crease, torn edge and shadow. sidelight-table-reference.png supplies the lighting atmosphere of an empty tabletop.
