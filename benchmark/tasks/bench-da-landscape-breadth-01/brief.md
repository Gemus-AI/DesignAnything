COMMON HARVEST | A shared rooftop garden for growing and gathering

Our apartment building has 12 residents who want to grow herbs and salad leaves together. Design COMMON HARVEST as a garden they can maintain in pairs, with six shared planting containers and a place for four people to sit. The roof measures 12000 × 8000 mm, an area of 96 m². Its existing concrete pavers, teal stair door and galvanized equipment plinth establish the setting. The parapet is 1100 mm high; the stair enclosure has 2400 mm clear headroom. The attached roof plan fixes the stair enclosure, supporting grid, equipment, tap and two drains.

The planting load zone occupies x = 6000–11000 mm and y = 2000–6500 mm, measured from the southwest corner. Its additional permanent load allowance is 300 kg/m²; the remaining terrace allowance is 150 kg/m². Each planting container has a 1200 × 600 mm footprint, a 350 mm body height, 180 mm of substrate and a saturated assembly weight of 144 kg, distributed across its full footprint. All additions use freestanding bases over protective mats. Retain a 500 mm perimeter access strip, 500 mm clear around the equipment and drains, and a continuous route at least 1200 mm wide from the stair door to planting, water, storage and seating. Plant 12 Thymus vulgaris, 12 Origanum vulgare and 18 Lactuca sativa. Print the site name as "COMMON HARVEST", name the two activity areas "Growing Table" and "Evening Bench", and put "Grow together, take a turn" on the maintenance station.

Audience: apartment residents and the building manager, for agreeing the garden layout, purchasing its components and assigning weekly care.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the site coordinates, load allowances, planting quantities, sunlight and maintenance records. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six deliverables with their proportions, formats and stages. roof-constraints.png establishes the roof boundary, supporting grid, load zone and fixed services. parapet-container-section.png establishes the parapet height, protective base arrangement and container envelope. existing-roof.png records the recognizable pavers, teal stair door and equipment plinth. spring-shared-planting.png provides context for residents planting together. summer-evening-pause.png provides context for a short evening gathering.
