CLEARWATER | Water use and hot-season resources report

Our community service centre needs a water-use report that residents can read while waiting at the counter and take home as an A4 summary. The annual series covers 2022–2026 in million m³/year. In 2026, Residential is 160.8, Industrial 78.6, Public 42.5 and Ecological 33.2; Total is 315.1. The hot-season tables add monthly Residential planning estimates for May–August in 2025 and 2026, measured in million m³/month. Label these estimates explicitly wherever they appear.

The visual direction is fixed around the existing authority mark in #1B6CA8. Sector colours are Residential #0072B2, Industrial #E69F00, Public #009E73 and Ecological #CC79A7. Pair them with circle, square, triangle and diamond markers respectively, and label sectors directly. Print "CLEARWATER" on every finished deliverable. Print "Water through the hot season" on the overview charts, resident summaries, social cards and exhibition board. Every chart carries a title, its unit, the credit "Source: CLEARWATER, 2026" and one short reading annotation. Use the attached tables for every displayed data value, including calculated percentages. Volume charts use zero-based axes; share diagrams use a separate percentage scale. The exhibition board is A3 landscape, the resident summaries are A4 portrait, and both have 15 mm clear margins.

Audience: community residents and public service staff, supporting discussion of annual demand, sector shares and hot-season residential demand.
