CURRENT HOURS | Adult swimwear from lane training to the waterfront

We are developing CURRENT HOURS for adults who swim regularly and spend time by the water afterwards. The range has four pieces: SW01, a training one-piece; SW02, a waist-length swim top; SW03, swim shorts with a 100 mm inseam; and CV01, a front-opening cover shirt with two pockets. Develop the silhouettes, seam divisions and pattern together, giving each piece a clear role in the same wardrobe. Sizes are S, M, L, XL and 2XL; the worn work features adults in S and 2XL.

Our material palette is fixed: F01 is 78% recycled nylon and 22% elastane, 190 g/m² warp knit with a chlorine-resistant finish; F02 is 100% polyester, 110 g/m² warp-knit lining; F03 is 100% polyester, 130 g/m² plain weave for the cover shirt. All three have a usable width of 1500 mm. The colours are deep teal #153C4A, clay #D97762 and chalk #F2EEE4. SW01 and SW02 have F01 outer fronts over F02 lining; SW01 and SW03 have lined seats and crotches. Chest and seat coverage continues through an overhead entry pose and a streamline pose. SW02 reaches the waist. The SW01 waist-to-leg-opening side measurement is 160 mm in S and 230 mm in 2XL. Print "CURRENT HOURS" and "Rinse after swimming" on each piece's internal label, with its size beneath.

Audience: the apparel lead, pattern cutter and merchandising team, for the range selection and sampling brief.
