ALDER TABLE | Menu photography for the Lemon Chicken Plate

Our lunch menu needs photographs of the Lemon Chicken Plate that let customers see exactly what arrives at the table. One serving contains 150 g cooked chicken breast cut into 3 slices, 120 g roasted potatoes comprising 4 halves, 80 g roasted carrots comprising 3 lengths, 1 lemon wedge and 20 g jus. The chicken overlaps across the centre, potatoes sit at 10 o'clock, carrots at 3 o'clock and the lemon at 6 o'clock; the jus pools beneath the chicken. Our existing ivory stoneware plate has a charcoal rim, a 260 mm outside diameter, a 200 mm inner well and a height of 24 mm. These ingredients and their positions define the dish throughout the set.

Use our dining room between 14:00 and 15:00, with diffuse light from the west window and a white reflector to the east. The tabletop is matte warm grey, #ECE7DE; retain the chicken's browned edges, potato skins and carrot surface texture. Complete views should make the portion easy to count. The printed menu page measures 180 × 270 mm with 3 mm bleed and a 150 × 100 mm photograph window. Print "ALDER TABLE", "Lemon Chicken Plate" and "One serving" on this page, together with our existing sprig emblem.

Audience: restaurant customers choosing a lunch dish from the printed menu or its accompanying ordering images.
