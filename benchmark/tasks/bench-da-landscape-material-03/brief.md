FIELDSTEP | A walking route through working rice terraces

Our farming cooperative is opening a short walking route beside four working rice terraces. FIELDSTEP occupies a rectangular 9,600 m² site, 120 m east to west and 80 m south to north. The direction is fixed: an out-and-back visitor route in the eastern strip, with three small observation nodes. The terraces, stone-faced risers, irrigation channel and western production lane remain in use throughout the project.

Working Terraces occupies 8,640 m²; Visitor Edge occupies 960 m² and is 12 m wide. Ground rises from elevation 100.00 m at the southern entrance to 106.00 m at the northern end, through terrace levels of 100.00, 102.00, 104.00 and 106.00 m. The existing production lane has a 4,000 mm clear width. The irrigation channel is 1,000 mm wide and 400 mm deep, with three existing drop structures and a 5,000 mm maintenance corridor. Visitor Edge contains three 4,000 × 4,000 mm node envelopes, centered at the coordinates in the site records, each accommodating six visitors. Use compacted crushed stone for level walking surfaces, textured timber for node decks and removable textile shade at the nodes. The existing plants are Oryza sativa, Juncus effusus and Persicaria hydropiper. Print the site name as "FIELDSTEP" on the masterplan, entrance panel and presentation cover; the entrance panel also carries "Fields at work".

Audience: terrace visitors and local farmers, with the cooperative using the work to agree the route, agricultural access and locations for visitor stops.
