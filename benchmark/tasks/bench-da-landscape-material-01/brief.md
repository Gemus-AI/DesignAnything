FIELD COURT | An existing courtyard renewed around four retained trees

Our community building opens onto a courtyard used for lunch breaks and brief conversations between activities. Renew its paths, seating and groundcover around the four existing Acer campestre trees. The site is 24 m east–west by 18 m north–south, an area of 432 m². The building forms the southern edge; weathered brick walls, 1800 mm high, enclose the other three sides. Entrances occupy the southernmost 2 m of both the west and east boundaries. A concrete strip spans the southern 2 m of the courtyard. Ground falls uniformly by 240 mm from west to east, a slope of 1%.

Coordinates start at the southwest corner, with x east and y north. T1 is at (6, 6) m, with trunk diameter 0.40 m and protection radius 2.40 m; T2 at (18, 6) m, diameter 0.45 m and radius 2.70 m; T3 at (6, 13) m, diameter 0.35 m and radius 2.10 m; T4 at (18, 13) m, diameter 0.40 m and radius 2.40 m. Retain these positions, complete protection circles, existing grades and leaf-litter surfaces within the circles. Place every new path, planting bed, pavement edge and bench footing outside them. Organise a continuous route between the entrances with 1800 mm clear width, and a gathering pocket with four benches, each 1800 mm long, 500 mm deep and 450 mm high. Provide a 900 × 1400 mm wheelchair space beside each of two benches. Use permeable clay pavers, timber slats on galvanised steel bench frames, and the groundcovers Geranium macrorrhizum and Luzula sylvatica. Print the site name as "FIELD COURT" and the two zone labels as "Garden Walk" and "Meeting Pocket" on the layout and schedule; repeat all three botanical names there, using P1 for Acer campestre, P2 for Geranium macrorrhizum and P3 for Luzula sylvatica.

Audience: community building users and the facilities team, choosing a courtyard arrangement for short stays, conversation and clear passage between entrances.
