SECOND BELL | Collaborative learning furniture from reclaimed school desk frames

Our community learning room needs furniture for six teenagers to work individually, gather in pairs for discussion and handwork, and clear the centre for a temporary class. Develop SECOND BELL around our six matching school desk frames, F01–F06. Each welded steel frame measures 600 mm wide × 450 mm deep × 700 mm high and uses 25 × 25 mm square tube. Retain every tube at its existing length and all 10 welded connections on each frame. The grey-green paint and local wear are part of the furniture's identity; use local rust removal and a clear water-based protective finish on the steel.

The added kit comprises six 660 × 500 × 18 mm birch plywood tops with 20 mm corner radii, six under-top birch plywood trays, 24 padded split-clamp assemblies attaching the tops to the frames, and six removable bridge couplers joining adjacent tops. Finish the plywood with clear water-based varnish. Each tray has external dimensions of 300 × 200 × 70 mm and 9 mm walls and base. Each bridge coupler fits within 120 × 60 × 35 mm and operates by hand, as do the frame clamps. Use two couplers per discussion pair; all six couplers have a place in the trays when detached. The room provides a clear 6000 × 4800 mm rectangle. The stored furniture must fit within a 2100 × 1100 mm floor area. Print "SECOND BELL" and "Built from six school desks" on the assembly sheet and both detail sheets.

Audience: the community room coordinator, teenage participants and the fabrication workshop, for choosing the working arrangement and preparing the furniture for fabrication.
