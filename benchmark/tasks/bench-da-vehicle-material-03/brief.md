ROVE S2 | A compact electric sports car for two

We are developing ROVE S2 for two people who enjoy driving and use the same car for daily journeys and weekends away. Design the complete exterior and cabin around our supplied electric platform: four wheels, two side-by-side seats, left-hand drive, two front-hinged side doors, a fixed roof and one rear luggage hatch. The design should feel contemporary or slightly ahead of the 2026 market, with purposeful surfaces and a clear relationship between the exterior, door openings and cockpit. Print "ROVE S2" on the rear badge and presentation cover, and "Two seats. One weekend." on the video end frame and presentation cover.

All dimensions are in mm. X points forward, Y vehicle-left and Z upward from ground; the rear axle is X=0. Wheelbase is 2400, both tracks are 1450, and all four tyres are 580 in outside diameter and 195 wide. The battery occupies X=450..1800, Y=-600..600, Z=180..330; the rear drive occupies X=-250..300, Y=-300..300, Z=250..550. The closed car, with mirrors unfolded, fits X=-750..3100, Y=-1020..1020, Z=0..1400. Body solids other than mirrors and tyres stay within Y=+/-890, with at least 140 ground clearance. Retain the supplied wheel exclusions, occupant and foot volumes, steering position and sight strips. The two H points are (1000,+/-360,520), the finished floor is Z=350, and the roof interior above the head envelopes is at least Z=1310. Each door opens to a labelled nominal 60 degrees, with a clear aperture at least 850 along X by 750 along Z and a sill top at most Z=480. Provide physical controls for turn signals, drive selection, hazards, emergency call, horn, front wipers, rear wipers, high beam and speed control. Carry L1 and L2 together behind the seats: each rigid case is 420 x 300 x 200, upright with its long axis parallel to X, with at least 10 clearance at each end, each side and above. Either case must pass through the same rear hatch while the other remains retained. Use muted lagoon-blue exterior panels, dark graphite pillars and wheel centres, warm charcoal seating and cockpit surfaces, and restrained copper-colour accents; select exact tones and specific material families in the opening specification.

Audience: our product lead, exterior and interior design teams, and packaging engineers, for the whole-car design decision and the next development brief.
