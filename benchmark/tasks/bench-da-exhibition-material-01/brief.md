THREAD LOGIC | A touring exhibition of textile archives and digital weaving

We are taking four archive textiles into small venues where visitors can follow the relationship between fibre, woven structure and digital pattern control. Build the exhibition around T01–T04, each 600 × 400 mm, and the three sections Fibre, Repeat and Code. T01 belongs to Fibre, T02 to Repeat, and T03–T04 to Code. Preserve the textile patterns in the attached collection images, including T03's 12 × 8 checkerboard and T04's 24 × 16 checkerboard. Print "THREAD LOGIC" and "From fibre to code" on the entrance graphic, video and presentation. Each object's ID, title and year travel together on the plans, object labels and presentation collection pages.

The same kit must fit Loom Hall, 10000 × 8000 mm with 3000 mm clear height, and Shuttle Room, 8000 × 6000 mm with 2700 mm clear height. Both have a 1400 mm south entrance, a 1400 mm east exit and reserved 1200 mm clear strips along the south and east walls. The logistics table fixes their loading doors, lifts and three transport crates. Develop four flat textile cases using aluminium frames, birch plywood supports and removable acrylic panels, with M6 bolts operated by a 5 mm hex key. Detached parts fit within 850 × 600 × 80 mm. Original textiles travel flat in four padded trays in C3; furniture, graphics and the single 13-inch screen travel in C1–C2. Set textile supports at 800 mm, case tops at 850 mm, label centres at 900 mm and graphic supports at 1800 mm. Originals have a 50 lux lighting limit; separately lit labels use 150 lux and 24 pt body text. Two 150 × 150 mm teaching replicas, R01 and R02, sit on touch ledges at 800 mm beside T01 and T04 respectively. The screen explains the supplied card-to-digital sequence. Each venue supplies two visitor seats. Plan for a 20-minute visit and 12 visitors at a time.

Audience: the archive team, exhibition fabricator and host venues will use the work to approve the touring kit and arrange its installation for the public and design students.
