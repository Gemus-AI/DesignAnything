HEAT THROUGH CLOUDS | Reading a satellite heat-island study

Our CIVIC CLIMATE DESK publication needs six finished graphics for a community discussion about the evidence behind urban heat reports. Use Yang and colleagues' 2024 study, Satellite Clear-Sky Observations Overestimate Surface Urban Heat Islands in Humid Cities. Its comparison covers 639 urban clusters worldwide during 2014–2016. The attached records contain the published global annual results, their 95% confidence intervals and the measurement conditions.

The story is how the available view changes the estimate. Explain the route from clear-sky satellite retrievals to the reconstructed all-sky comparison, with surface heat-island intensity defined as urban minus rural land surface temperature. Our existing publication style uses #D55E00 circles for observation-based estimates, #0072B2 diamonds for reconstructed estimates and black capped lines for confidence intervals. Print "HEAT THROUGH CLOUDS" and "Land surface temperature" on every graphic. The meeting uses a 2400 mm × 1350 mm projection surface; the takeaway is A4, 210 mm × 297 mm; the two portrait explainers use the 9:16 phone carrier with a 5% inset on every edge.
