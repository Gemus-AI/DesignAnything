NOTEFORM | Discovery, refill and storage packaging for personal fragrance

We are developing NOTEFORM for people who want to try small quantities of fragrance and build combinations they can return to. Our six fragrances are F01 CITRUS SHADE, F02 LINEN AIR, F03 CEDAR STILL, F04 FIG PATH, F05 IRIS DUSK and F06 SALT LIGHT. The existing container is a closed 2 mL spray vial with a clear cylindrical glass body and matte charcoal cap: diameter 12 mm, total height 60 mm, cap height 14 mm, straight labeling zone height 30 mm and paper label 28 by 30 mm. Packaging structure, typography, colors and materials are open. Single cartons S01–S06 each hold their corresponding fragrance and stay within 38 wide by 32 deep by 82 high mm. Discovery and refill packs each hold three vials and stay within 96 by 42 by 82 mm. P01 FIRST NOTES contains F01/F02/F03; P02 AFTER HOURS contains F04/F05/F06; P03 GREEN WALK contains F01/F03/F04; P04 OPEN AIR contains F02/F05/F06, one vial of each. R01 CITRUS SHADE REFILL contains three F01 vials; R03 CEDAR STILL REFILL contains three F03 vials. Every three-vial pack contains 3 x 2 mL = 6 mL.

The discovery carrier should become a useful place to keep the collection. Give every discovery pack a physical display opening that exposes an identifying part of all three vial labels while the closed carrier retains and separates the vials. Develop a reversible physical connection or holder for P01 and R01, with separate opening access, within a combined envelope of 192 wide by 84 deep by 90 high mm. Its complete stock is six filled vials: F01/F02/F03 in P01 and three F01 in R01. Refill means replacing a matching empty position with a sealed vial. The counter fixture fits within 360 wide by 240 deep by 260 high mm. Print "NOTEFORM" on vial labels, pack fronts and display headers. Vial labels also carry fragrance code, name and 2 mL. Single fronts carry fragrance code/name, "Single trial" and 1 x 2 mL; discovery fronts carry the pack name, "Discovery set" and 3 x 2 mL; refill fronts carry the refill name, fragrance code, "Refill set" and 3 x 2 mL. Discovery panels identify all three contained fragrances by code and name. Rear panels carry the corresponding supplied scent descriptions and both safety sentences in the fact sheet; refill rears also carry its two replacement instructions. Build a six-fragrance identification system using readable names and codes alongside the selected colors or symbols.

Audience: the brand owner, packaging designer and retail team, choosing a packaging family for small-quantity fragrance discovery, retained storage and replacement sales.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the fixed vial geometry, packaging limits, stock relationships and exact printed copy. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists every deliverable with its proportion, format and stage. 03-fragrances.md contains the six fragrance codes, names and scent descriptions. 04-combinations.md contains the discovery and refill pack names, contents and volumes. bottle-envelope.png is the identity image for the existing closed vial and blank label area. packaging-constraints.png is the constraint drawing for vial dimensions and maximum packaging, grouping and display envelopes. fragrance-counter-reference.png is an atmosphere reference for trying and arranging small fragrance vials at home.
