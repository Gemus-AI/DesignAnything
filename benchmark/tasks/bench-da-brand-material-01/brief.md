COMMON PANTRY | Two branches of one neighbourhood cooperative

We run COMMON PANTRY, a neighbourhood grocery cooperative, and need a branch identity system for Market Green and River Lane. Both shops welcome members and non-members. The supplied basket symbol and "COMMON PANTRY" wordmark form our complete master logo. Preserve its lettering, symbol, arrangement, proportions and deep green ink, nominal #244E3D. Use the complete master at a uniform scale on a light solid background, with branch cues placed outside its visible bounds. The two shops should read as branches of the same cooperative, with their names providing the clearest distinction.

Market Green has a 4800 × 800 mm fascia and four departments, in this directory order: Fresh produce; Bread & dairy; Pantry staples; Household refills. Its hours are Daily 08:00–20:00. River Lane has a 2400 × 600 mm fascia and three departments, in this order: Fresh produce; Bread & dairy; Pantry staples. Its hours are Daily 09:00–19:00. Household refills belongs to Market Green. Bag fronts carry the exact line "Good food. Shared ownership.". Membership card fronts carry the exact heading "Cooperative member" and sample field "Member 0042". Window notices carry the exact sentence "Everyone is welcome. Membership is optional.".

Audience: neighbourhood residents and cooperative members need to recognise their branch and its offer, while the cooperative team needs to approve a consistent system for both shops.
