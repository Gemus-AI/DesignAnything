CHINA RESIDENCE ATLAS | Registration and residence across China's provinces

Urban Ledger is commissioning an atlas for readers who want to understand the relationship between household registration and current residence in China. The national census figures at 2020-11-01 are 124,837,153 cross-province residents and 250,979,606 within-province residents. The provincial layer comprises our ten selected pair estimates, totaling 24,300,000 people, ranked within that selection. Present national figures and pair estimates as separate statistical layers, with provincial comparisons calculated from the selected pairs.

Our publication style is fixed: white ground, black typography, blue #0072B2 for cross-province figures and orange #E69F00 for within-province figures, with direct labels supplementing color. Build the atlas on the attached 31-province schematic grid. Each connection runs from household-registration province to current-residence province and represents a relationship between those places. Print "CHINA RESIDENCE ATLAS" on the reader-facing pieces, "Residents at 1 November 2020" as their time label, and "Selected-pair estimates" beside the provincial layer. The release comprises landscape web panels, a portrait phone explainer and an A4 summary.

Audience: the general public and urban research readers, with Urban Ledger's editorial team using the work to approve the atlas for publication.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the publication identity, palette and statistical framing. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists every deliverable with its proportion, format and stage. 03-population-records.en.md contains national figures, ten pair estimates, calculated subtotals, field definitions and source credits. 04-province-grid.en.md contains the 31 province names, atlas join keys and schematic positions. layout-constraints.png supplies the web, phone and A4 carrier geometry. province-grid.png supplies the fixed schematic placement of the provinces. publication-style.png supplies Urban Ledger's existing typography, rules and chart treatment. reader-desk.png supplies the atmosphere of a reader moving between a printed report and a phone.
