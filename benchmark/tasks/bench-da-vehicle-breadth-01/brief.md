CAMPUS LOOP | A complete campus parcel vehicle

We run a campus parcel service and need one complete low-speed delivery vehicle for students, staff and our loading team. The name "CAMPUS LOOP" belongs on both vehicle sides. Develop contemporary bodywork, wheel covers, compartment doors and an integrated status area around the supplied six-wheel platform. The closed vehicle fits within 1000 L x 640 W x 900 H mm. Each wheel is 160 mm in diameter and 50 mm wide; hub positions and the chassis module are fixed in client-hardpoints.en.md. Use powder-coated aluminium for chassis covers, glass-fibre composite for body and door panels, and thermoplastic polyurethane for perimeter bumpers. The roof sensor's full perimeter band at z 810–840 mm stays exposed through every door movement; the front and rear sensor windows each retain their 80 W x 60 H mm opening and 50 mm inward clearance.

Three upright parcels travel together: P-A, 180 L x 140 W x 100 H mm; P-B, 240 L x 180 W x 160 H mm; and P-C, 160 L x 120 W x 80 H mm. Each has a separate locked compartment, labelled A, B or C, with independent right-side access and at least 10 mm clearance to every wall and the closed door. Arrange them alongside one another, with their height vertical. A small physical pickup panel on the right accepts code 2476 and releases B alone. The main status area pairs each exact message with a distinct symbol: TRAVEL uses "DELIVERING", YIELD uses "I'M WAITING", PICKUP READY uses "ENTER CODE", AUTH B uses "COLLECT B", LOADING uses "LOADING", and OUT OF SERVICE uses "OUT OF SERVICE". After P-B is removed and B closes and locks, the message returns to ENTER CODE. Collection takes place in a 1600 x 1400 mm bay beside a separate 1200 mm pedestrian strip; the vehicle origin stays at bay coordinates x 300 / y 350 mm, facing +x, throughout collection. Door travel stays inside the bay.

Audience: students and staff collecting parcels, and operators loading and maintaining the fleet, for the vehicle design decision and review of cargo access and public communication.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the vehicle, parcel, state and collection records. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six deliverables with their proportions, formats and stages. client-hardpoints.en.md contains the fixed coordinates, sensor allowances, parcel dimensions and bay geometry. platform-and-bay.png maps the platform, sensor envelopes and collection footprint. parcel-inventory.png identifies the three existing parcel forms that the compartments accommodate. campus-path-reference.png provides the campus setting and pedestrian atmosphere.
