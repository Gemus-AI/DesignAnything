HEIRLOOM HOUR | Archive lace for a contemporary personal ceremony

We are commissioning one dress for adults marking a personal anniversary at home with family. The lace carries the family's textile memory; the silhouette should feel comfortable at a dining table and composed when walking across the room. Develop a calf-length dress with elbow-length sleeves and a hem 100 mm below the knee. The neckline, volume and fastening are open to your design. Print "HEIRLOOM HOUR" and "Archive lace 01" on the inside neck label.

Our available archive lace is one usable rectangle, 900 mm wide by 2400 mm long: ivory, 70% cotton and 30% polyamide, warp-knitted, 100 g/m². Its repeat is 60 mm across the width by 80 mm along the length. Each upright motif has one round central eyelet, six oval petal eyelets, a downward stem and two pointed leaves, one on each side. Preserve that motif, its orientation and repeat spacing throughout. The dress has three transparency windows: two front upper-sleeve panels, each 100 × 120 mm, in lace over silk organza; and one centred upper-back panel, 120 × 80 mm, in lace alone, starting 40 mm below the back neckline. The remaining garment area from neckline to hem is opaque. Use ivory 100% cupro plain weave, 90 g/m², for opaque backing and ivory 100% silk organza, 24 g/m², for the two sleeve windows. Lace placement across the opaque areas is open.

Audience: the wearer and our dressmaker, for choosing the finished silhouette and agreeing the lace placement before cutting the archive material.
