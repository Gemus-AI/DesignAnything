LEDGE TO LIFE | An intertidal photography story at West Ledge

We are commissioning a photographic observation feature for visitors to our coastal learning centre. The title is "LEDGE TO LIFE" and the opening line is "A shore at three scales". Work within the West Ledge study area, 20000 mm east–west by 12000 mm north–south, during the session on 18 September 2026, 08:30–09:50 BST. Low water is at 09:10 BST, 0.8 m above chart datum. The story moves from the shore to two microhabitats and then to the shells within them.

Our two subjects are L1, a Common limpet, Patella vulgata, with a 32 mm shell at S1, and P1, a Common periwinkle, Littorina littorea, with an 18 mm shell height at S2. L1 has a grey-white conical shell with radiating ribs; P1 has a dark grey-brown pointed shell with six whorls and a lighter apex. Their appearance and sample locations carry through the series. Photograph both in their existing positions using diffuse ambient daylight, with an initial setting of f/8 and 1/250 s. Camera position and composition are yours to develop. Captions describe the visible shell, substrate and water conditions alongside the supplied records.

Audience: nature observers and the coastal learning centre's editorial team, choosing an observation feature that helps visitors relate small organisms to their surroundings.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the project facts and fixed wording. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md contains the proportions, formats and stages for all 14 files. 03-site-records.en.md contains the study boundary, sample coordinates and tide context. 04-observation-records.en.md contains the specimen names, measurements and visible characteristics. west-ledge-plan.png locates both samples within the study area and marks the session window. caption-crop-geometry.png defines the image inset and caption zone across three proportions. limpet-identity.png records L1's shell appearance. periwinkle-identity.png records P1's shell appearance. field-ruler.png records the 50 mm measuring ruler and its graduations. coastal-light-reference.png provides empty-shore daylight and wet-rock atmosphere.

2. Two shore photographs, W1 establishing West Ledge and W2 locating S1 and S2 within the same shore

4. Two whole-organism photographs, O1 of L1 and O2 of P1, each with a calibrated 5 mm scale bar
5. Two shell-detail photographs, D1 of L1's radiating ribs and D2 of P1's spiral surface, each with a calibrated 1 mm scale bar
