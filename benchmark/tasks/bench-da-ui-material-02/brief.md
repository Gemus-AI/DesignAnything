RIDGE MINI | Cycling computer screens for riding, navigation and pause

We need five finished interface screens and a final operating reference for RIDGE Mini, our three-button cycling computer. Riders need to pick out speed, the next turn and recording status during a brief glance at the handlebars. The portrait display has an active area of 48 × 64 mm inside a 64 × 90 mm body, starting 8 mm from the left and top edges. The screen uses opaque black #000000 and opaque white #FFFFFF; interaction is through short presses of P, R and B.

P changes between DASHBOARD and NAVIGATION, R toggles RECORDING and PAUSED, and B toggles the backlight. These meanings carry through every page and alert. The five supplied snapshots set the exact readings and wording. Print "RIDGE Mini" and the heading "Button-and-state quick reference" on the operating reference.

Audience: cyclists who need quick readings, and our product and interface teams choosing the screen layouts and operating reference for release.
