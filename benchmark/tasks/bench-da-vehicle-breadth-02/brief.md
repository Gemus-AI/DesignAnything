SLEEPLINK | A two-person sleeper compartment from conversation to lights out

We are developing compartment C02 for two adult night-train travellers, A and B. We need a contemporary interior that gives them somewhere to talk during the day and two separate places to sleep at night. Explore three genuinely different arrangements, select one, and carry its furniture and passenger identities through the complete design. The clear shell measures 2800 mm along the train, 2200 mm from corridor wall to window wall and 2450 mm high. Coordinates run from x=0–2800, y=0–2200 and z=0–2450. The adjacent corridor occupies y=-900 to -100 and has 800 mm clear width; its partition occupies y=-100 to 0. The doorway is at x=1900–2600, z=0–2050. Its 700 mm sliding leaf travels left into the pocket at x=1200–1900. Preserve the fixed window at y=2200, x=450–1850, z=1050–1900 and the solid service block at x=2450–2800, y=1850–2200, z=0–450. Both states need the 800 by 800 mm entrance landing at x=1900–2700, y=0–800 and a continuous route at least 550 mm wide to each passenger place.

Day D has two seats and a shared table with at least 600 by 400 mm usable surface, 680–760 mm above the floor. Each seat needs a 480 by 950 mm minimum seated-use envelope, including at least 450 mm seat depth and 500 mm front leg space; seat height is 420–480 mm with at least 1100 mm clear height above it. Night N has separate A and B sleeping surfaces, each at least 1900 by 700 mm with 850 mm minimum clear height above; at least one is 350–550 mm above the floor. Each traveller brings a 550 by 350 by 230 mm bag, a 550 by 350 by 120 mm pillow and a 450 by 350 by 180 mm folded quilt. Store both bags in both states and both bedding sets in D; stow the table in N. A must be able to retrieve BAG-A at night while BAG-B and B's sleeping surface stay in place. Give each traveller an independently controlled reading light, a 200 by 150 mm clear shelf and a location for a 170 by 85 by 12 mm phone. Print "READING / OFF" at each light control, "SLEEPLINK / C02" beside the corridor doorway and "C02" on the inside door identification. Use SLEEPLINK in every board title and on the presentation cover. Develop separate window and door privacy controls, including their hardware and stow positions, and select named materials and finishes for the walls, upholstery, table and grips.

Audience: the interior design lead and product team, choosing the compartment arrangement and the details to carry into development for travellers who need sleep, luggage access and brief conversation.
