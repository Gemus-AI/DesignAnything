LOOP AGAIN | Refurbished handheld listings that make condition clear

We sell refurbished gaming handhelds through our own storefront. Build a product image system that helps buyers choose between two individual Pocket P1 units by seeing the condition and the bundle together. Print "LOOP AGAIN" and "See the wear. Know the bundle." on the finished layouts. Where directions are requested, compare three approaches to organising this information, select one, and carry that approach through the listing.

The stock is PA-101, Pocket P1 Pearl, shell #E8E5DE, Grade A, USD 99; and PA-102, Pocket P1 Graphite, shell #343A40, Grade B, USD 79. Both have 64 GB storage and measure 160 × 76 × 18 mm. PA-101 has a 6 mm horizontal hairline on the upper-right rear shell and comes with one USB-C cable and one zip case. PA-102 has a 12 mm diagonal scratch on the upper-right rear shell, a polished lower-right face button and a 3 mm scuff on the right lip of the USB-C port; its bundle contains one USB-C cable. These marks remain visible at their recorded locations whenever those surfaces appear. Use the fixed labels "Unit", "Condition", "Included" and "Price" for the purchase information. Square product cards, portrait shopping panels and landscape detail panels share the same product facts. The dimension comparison uses a plain 85 × 54 mm card beside the handheld.

Audience: budget-conscious buyers comparing wear and accessories, with the merchandising team choosing the listing system for these two units.
