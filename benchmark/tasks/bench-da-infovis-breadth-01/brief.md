SOLAR SYSTEM MISSIONS | Mission timelines and target connections

ORBIT DESK is commissioning a museum reading set about six missions: Cassini, MESSENGER, Rosetta, BepiColombo, DART and Hera. Readers should be able to move from when a mission operated to what it studied, then find the same mission in a closer comparison. The editorial snapshot is 2026-09-12. The records contain five target bodies and seven mission-to-target links; the timeline runs from 1997-01-01 to 2027-01-01.

Use our existing masthead and editorial style. Print "SOLAR SYSTEM MISSIONS" on all six pieces. Planet missions use #0072B2, comet missions #CC79A7 and asteroid missions #E69F00, paired with mission IDs and written category labels. Dates use UTC and retain their recorded precision: BepiColombo's planned Mercury orbit insertion is 2026-11-21; Hera's planned system rendezvous is 2026-11. Separate actual and planned milestones into labeled tracks. Organize target connections as a two-column relationship diagram, with the caption "Connections indicate mission targets". The physical reading surfaces are a 420 × 280 mm landscape panel, a 200 × 300 mm portrait panel and an A4 takeaway, each with a 10 mm clear margin.

Audience: museum visitors and general science readers, choosing which missions and destinations to explore further through the exhibition and its takeaway.
