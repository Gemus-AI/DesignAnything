LANTERN TRANSIT | Overnight rail closure and replacement bus notices

We need six finished artworks to help Lantern Transit passengers change routes during track renewal. L1 is Indigo, #2856A3, with the normal station order West Dock — Alder Gate — Civic Hall — Foundry — East Market — Garden End. From 17 October 2027 at 22:00 until 18 October 2027 at 05:00, L1 has no trains between Alder Gate and East Market in either direction. Trains continue between West Dock and Alder Gate and between East Market and Garden End, both ways; Civic Hall and Foundry have no rail service. This is one overnight closure, with rail service planned to resume on 18 October 2027 at 05:00. All times are local and use the 24-hour clock.

B1 runs throughout that interval, serving Alder Gate — Exit 2, Stop A; Civic Hall — South Stop C; Foundry — East Stop F; and East Market — Exit 1, Stop E, in that order toward East Market and the reverse order toward Alder Gate. It serves all four stops in both directions, with Alder Gate and East Market as its endpoints. Print "Lantern Transit", "free replacement bus" and "every 12 minutes" on every artwork, alongside L1 and B1. The 12 minutes is the planned bus frequency. Normal rail fares apply on the operating rail sections. Passengers at Alder Gate use Exit 2 and Stop A toward East Market; passengers at East Market use Exit 1 and Stop E toward Alder Gate. The notices meet passengers at a ticket gate and a platform column, the route sheets support close reading, and the mobile versions support a quick decision while approaching the station.

Audience: commuters changing their usual route, deciding where rail service ends and which B1 stop and direction to use.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the network, closure, bus operations, fixed wording and reading conditions. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six deliverables with their proportions, formats and stages. 03-existing-network.png is the identity input for the existing six-station order and Indigo L1 treatment. 04-reading-positions.png is the constraint drawing for the ticket-gate notice, platform-column notice, route sheets and mobile reading conditions. 05-morning-concourse.png is an atmosphere reference for passenger movement and divided attention at a busy ticket gate.

2. Two A4 portrait replacement-route sheets, one toward East Market and one toward Alder Gate, each showing the six-station network, the closed rail section, both operating rail sections and all four named B1 stops in its travel order
