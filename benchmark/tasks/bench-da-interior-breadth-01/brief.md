INTERVAL CINEMA | Quiet pause room beside the screening corridor

We are fitting out an existing room at Interval Cinema for visitors who need a short pause before or after a screening. Its internal dimensions are 8.0 m east–west by 6.0 m north–south, with a 3.0 m clear ceiling. The origin is the inside southwest floor corner; x runs east, y north and z upward. D1 is the open south-wall entrance at x=1.2–2.4, z=0–2.2, measuring 1.2 m wide by 2.2 m high. W1 is the north-wall window at x=5.2–7.2, z=1.0–2.5, measuring 2.0 m wide by 1.5 m high with a 1.0 m sill. C1 occupies x=3.8–4.2, y=3.3–3.7, z=0–3: a full-height 0.4 m square column. Retain this envelope and these openings; D1 stays open. New nonstructural partitions and an adjustable interior shade at W1 are available to the design.

Arrange two individual resting places, S1 and S2, and one two-person conversation place, P1: four seats altogether. Give each individual place a visual buffer from D1 and P1 while keeping it connected to the room. Provide three reachable belongings spots, one beside each place; P1's spot holds two bags. Connect all places to D1 with a continuous clear route at least 1.2 m wide and a connected turning circle at least 1.5 m in diameter. The corridor remains active, and W1 supplies the room's daylight. Address these conditions with visible acoustic treatment near the south boundary, adjustable shading, diffuse electric light and a visitor brightness control. Choose the furniture, palette and cleanable contact surfaces. Print "INTERVAL CINEMA" on the four drawing and specification sheets; place "Quiet pause" and "Cinema corridor" at D1. The two visitor views use camera A at (0.8,2.2,1.6) toward (4.8,4.5,1.3) and camera B at (7.3,5.2,1.6) toward (2.4,1.4,1.2), in metres. A shows one visitor at S1; B shows all four seats occupied.

Audience: visitors needing a quieter interval and their companions are the users; the cinema operator and fit-out team will use the work to choose the room arrangement and brief installation.
