NEST T2 | A two-person weekend touring trailer

We are developing NEST T2 for two adults taking short weekend trips. Design the whole trailer: exterior, interior, cold-meal preparation, luggage storage and the conversion between daytime use and sleeping. The platform is fixed; the body shape and cabin layout are open. Work within the 4800 x 2400 x 2450 mm closed total envelope and the 3600 x 2100 mm shell envelope at Z=450..2450 mm. Retain the single axle, two main tyres, A-frame endpoints, coupling reference at X=2850, Y=0, Z=450 mm, four stabilizer attachments and retractable jockey wheel. Coordinates use X forward, Y trailer-left and Z up. The attached coordinate tables locate every fixed box and point.

The cabin needs exactly two day seats, a 650 x 500 mm usable meal table, an internal cold-meal preparation surface and a flat bed at least 1950 x 1400 mm. Provide a positive-Y side entry with at least 600 x 1650 mm clear aperture, a folding two-tread step at Z=180 and 360 mm, an opening window and a high ventilation opening. The eleven portable items E1-E5, P1/P2, R1/R2 and C1/C2 each need their own retained travel location. Develop TRAVEL, PARK and NIGHT around the same shell and furniture, with DAY as the parked seating arrangement. PARK, NIGHT and pack-up use the attached 6150 x 5300 mm campsite and its 3100 mm height ceiling. Use warm off-white for the shell, muted forest green for its lower protective band, graphite running gear, light natural wood inside and warm grey cushions. Proposed materials are aluminium-faced composite shell panels, a steel underframe, birch-faced plywood furniture, recycled PET cushion upholstery and anti-slip rubber flooring. Print "NEST T2" and "Room for the weekend" on the entry-side exterior; label E2 "FRESH" and E3 "WASTE".

Audience: our product team and fabrication partners, for selecting the integrated trailer design and preparing the next development brief for two-person weekend use.
