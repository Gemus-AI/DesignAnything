DAY MILE | Running shoes for the hours around a run

We are launching DAY MILE to people who fit running around work, errands and the journey home. Build a photography campaign around the same running-shoe model worn by Alex Lane and Morgan Reed in everyday city settings. The shoe has an off-white #F4F1EA upper and an orange #FF5A1F sole accent; its construction and markings follow the three attached shoe views. Use the existing black stroke mark from logo.png. Print "DAY MILE" and "Your city. Your pace." on the advertising images and video end frame.

Alex's appearance and outfit follow alex-lane.png. Morgan's appearance and outfit follow morgan-reed.png. Photograph preparation, easy running and recovery in the first 60 minutes after sunrise. The shared lighting treatment is southeast sunlight at approximately 20 degrees elevation, a 4:1 key-to-fill ratio and 5200 K white balance. The finished campaign uses landscape 3:2, square 1:1 and vertical 9:16 compositions. Advertising copy sits within the upper quarter; faces, both shoes and copy remain inside an 8% inset from every edge. In whole-product photographs, the pair spans at least 80% of the image width.

Audience: ordinary urban runners considering their next everyday running shoe, with our brand and media teams selecting the launch imagery and placements.
