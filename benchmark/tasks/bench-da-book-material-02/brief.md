PARK LEAVES | A pocket guide to six trees in British parks

We publish pocket guides for urban nature observers. PARK LEAVES brings six historical botanical plates into a handbook readers can consult beside a tree. Print "PARK LEAVES" and "Six trees for British parks" on the front cover. The six entries are Horse chestnut, Rowan, Norway maple, English oak, Silver birch and Beech, using b1.jpg through b6.jpg respectively. Identification starts with leaf structure or shape, then checks a second visible feature; readers should understand how each route reaches its entry.

The handbook has 12 pages, including its covers, at a nominal closed size of 105 × 148 mm. Production is 300 copies, four-colour digital printing on 140 gsm uncoated paper, saddle stitched, with a 0 mm allocated spine panel and exterior wording on the front and back panels. The cover is page 1; how-to and key occupy pages 2–3; b1–b6 occupy pages 4–9 in order; the index/source spread is pages 10–11; comparison is page 12, the back cover. Use a two-column page grid with 8 mm margins and a 4 mm gutter. Print "Observe only — do not taste or collect" on page 2 and pages 4–9. The content follows the attached leaf, second-feature and observation-season records; the six supplied plates provide the botanical imagery.

Audience: urban nature observers using the guide outdoors, and our editorial and production team deciding whether its identification routes and portable layout are ready for publication.
