ALDER YARD | Shade and outdoor learning for a school courtyard

Our school needs a courtyard that can support a lesson and remain useful when the bell releases pupils outside. The site is 40 m east–west by 30 m north–south, totaling 1,200 m². The existing north-side school building occupies 192 m²; the landscape area is 1,008 m². Retain its footprint, cream brickwork, teal window frames and four existing Celtis australis trees. Ground level rises 300 mm from south to north. The east-edge maintenance passage stays 4 m wide.

Work from the fixed Assembly Court boundary and the six zone areas in the site records. A class comprises 30 pupils and one teacher; Learning Terrace needs six five-pupil tables and one teacher position, reached from door D1 along a route no longer than 35 m and at least 1.8 m wide. Provide at least 90 m² of geometric shade within the 120 m² Assembly Court at 12:30 on 15 July. Combine a planted grove, shrub and groundcover layers, and an overhead canopy; retain an open lawn and provide 120 m² of permeable concrete. The canopy envelope is 12 m by 10 m, with 3,000 mm minimum clear height and 4,500 mm maximum overall height. Print "ALDER YARD", all six zone names and all three plant names on the masterplan and materials and planting schedule. Use "Geometric shade study" as the shade drawing title.

Audience: pupils and teachers using the courtyard during lessons and breaks, with the school leadership and grounds team choosing the layout and maintenance approach.
