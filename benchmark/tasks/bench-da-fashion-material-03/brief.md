COMMON SHIFT | Inclusive workshop uniforms from a fixed block

Our workshop team assembles storage units at benches, shelves and floor level. Develop jacket J01 and trousers T01 from our existing block for fit profiles A, B and C. The jacket has a stand collar, set-in sleeves and a centre-front zip; the trousers have straight legs, an elasticated waistband and a zipped fly. Retain this construction while developing the elbow and knee panels, access details and colour blocking. The attached records give separate body measurements, finished garment dimensions and task positions for each profile.

Use fabric F01 throughout: 65% polyester and 35% cotton, 245 g/m², 1500 mm usable width, 2/1 twill with a preshrunk finish. Main panels are #263C46; elbow and knee panels are #B8C3BD. Each outfit has four pockets: two lower jacket pockets and two outer-thigh pockets, each with a 180 mm opening and 220 mm usable depth. Silver reflective tape is 50 mm wide, with one horizontal band around the jacket and one around each trouser leg. Print "COMMON SHIFT" on the inside back-neck label and "WORKSHOP 04" on the existing 90 × 45 mm left-chest badge, preserving its three-step emblem.

Audience: workshop staff, the clothing buyer and the garment maker, choosing a shared uniform and the three fits to take into sampling.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the fixed garment, material and identification facts. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the deliverables, proportions, formats and stages. 03-fit-and-task-records.en.md contains the body measurements, garment dimensions, placement coordinates and assigned movements. base-block.png records the existing construction and dimensioned B block alongside the three body profiles. task-positions.png defines the three working poses. workshop-badge.png supplies the existing badge and reflective tape sample. assembly-workshop.png provides the working environment, lighting and material atmosphere.
