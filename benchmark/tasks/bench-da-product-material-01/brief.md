DAYREST | Screen-free bedside wake light

We are developing a complete bedside wake light for urban residents who want to put their screens away before bed and still have an independent morning alarm. The product name is "DAYREST"; print it on the lamp where it is legible in the finished exterior views. Build around one each of the seven supplied modules, L01, A01, C01, P01, E01, B01 and M01. The complete lamp, including feet and controls, fits within 180 W × 150 D × 210 H mm. We need you to resolve its enclosure, support, light path and control arrangement. Use a frosted PC diffuser, a woven polyester speaker covering and a fine-textured matte PC/ABS housing.

Operation is screen-free and self-contained, using physical controls, light cues and requested spoken setting feedback. E01 is a rotary push control; B01 needs a tactile contact area at least 28 mm across its shortest usable dimension. Print the M01 mode legends "OFF", "LIGHT" and "LIGHT + TONE" in left-to-right order. An armed wake sequence starts 20 minutes before the stored time; B01 provides a 9-minute snooze and a 2-second hold to dismiss the current occurrence. The bedside top is 400 W × 300 D mm, 520 mm above the floor, with the bed on its right. Its lamp region is x=180..380, y=60..240; the rear cable strip is y=240..300. Controls face the right/front hand approach, the USB connection remains accessible from the rear, and the diffuser shields the bare LED face from the bed-level view.

Audience: our product lead and industrial design team, for selecting the complete lamp design to take into enclosure development.
