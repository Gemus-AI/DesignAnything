NEARLINK | Low-floor community shuttle exterior and interior

We run NEARLINK between neighbourhood service points and need one coordinated exterior and passenger interior. The body envelope is 7000 L by 2200 W by 3000 H mm, wheelbase 4000 mm and wheel diameter 650 mm. The right-side passenger doorway occupies x=1900–3100, with a 1200 mm clear opening above the 320 mm passenger floor. Develop contemporary surfaces, glazing and lighting around these fixed positions. Retain the operator's existing symbol of three vertically linked open circles beside the printed name "NEARLINK" on the front and boarding side.

The near-door seating group makes this vehicle useful to different passengers. N8 offers six fixed seats and two deployed tip-up seats, T1 and T2; F6 folds both tip-ups for a 1000 by 600 mm stroller space and a separate 500 by 500 mm caregiver space. Both arrangements retain the 750 mm clear aisle. Boarding and alighting use the same right-side door and 1100 mm wide bridge, which reaches 700 mm from the 320 mm floor to the 300 mm curb. Give passengers physical STOP request buttons, 35 mm diameter support rails with 38 mm adjacent-surface clearance, and a specified support-to-background light reflectance value difference of at least 30 points. Print "LET PASSENGERS EXIT FIRST" at the door and "FOLD BOTH SEATS BEFORE STROLLER PARKING" at the flexible bay. Use recycled PET seat fabric and nonslip rubber flooring. Colourway A combines #E8E6DD with #1A655E; B changes the accent to #655168. A and route R1 are the defaults; the attached records define both routes and cases E1–E4.

Audience: the operator's fleet and service teams and the vehicle design partner, deciding how older passengers, caregivers with strollers and ordinary passengers share the shuttle and its boarding space.
