FOLD PARADE | Paper puppet band rhythm game

We are building FOLD PARADE for players who enjoy music and simple controls. Design our three-piece paper puppet band and its first playable performance at Boxlight Stage: Pip on drums, Reed on bass and Wren on keyboard. Their appearances are yours to develop. Our existing folded-fan emblem anchors the identity. The set and performers should visibly use cardstock edges, folds, cut joints and shadows cast by stage lighting. Pip's signature instrument is the Tin Moon Drum.

The performance uses Pocket Overture, a 4-second passage at 120 BPM in 4/4, supplied as a note and event record. There is one right-to-left tap lane. The camera uses a 1920 × 1080 logical coordinate system, with an 80-unit safe inset. Band performance occupies y=200–650; the input strip occupies x=160–1760, y=720–960. Its judgment line is x=960. Notes travel at 400 units per second, become visible 300 ms before their target and accept input within ±120 ms. The corresponding judgment band is 96 units wide. Cue symbols measure at least 64 units; printed interface text measures at least 26 units in character height.

Use blue #1E4D8A with a hollow diamond for "Ready", green #236344 with a checked circle for "Hit", and red #A33232 with a crossed split square for "Miss", against cream #FFF5DF. Controller and touch prompts read "Press A" and "Tap" respectively. Print "FOLD PARADE" on all six final images; gameplay headers also carry "Boxlight Stage" and "Tin Moon Drum". The three gameplay moments are 1750 ms, 2540 ms and 3140 ms, with score/combo values of 0/0, 200/2 and 200/0 respectively.

Audience: our art director and gameplay team, choosing the band's appearance and approving the visual assets for the first playable song.
