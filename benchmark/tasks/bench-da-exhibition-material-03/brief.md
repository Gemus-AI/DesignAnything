CEDAR STREET VOICES | A travelling exhibition of neighbourhood photographs and personal memories

Our Alder Ward Memory Archive is taking six archive items into two community venues: three photographs, P01–P03, and three interview excerpts, T01–T03. Design CEDAR STREET VOICES as a walk through three places on Cedar Street, with each photograph beside the memory associated with that place. The sequence is fixed: Morning trade, After school, A place to pause. Print "CEDAR STREET VOICES" and "One street, many memories" on the introductory panel and presentation cover. Treat each interview as its speaker's recollection, introduced by the exact label "Personal memory"; introduce photographic records with "Archive photograph".

The pairings are P01 Market corner, 1968, with T01 Ruth Bell, interviewed 2024-04-12; P02 Bus stop, 1989, with T02 David Cole, interviewed 2024-04-19; and P03 Corner bench, 2007, with T03 Leila Grant, interviewed 2024-05-03. The six items have fixed captions and excerpt text in the archive records. Their display sizes are 300 × 200 mm for each photograph and 210 × 297 mm for each transcript. All six appear at both venues. Residents usually spend 10–15 minutes here; operators expect up to 12 visitors at once.

Market Room has a clear interior of 8000 × 6000 × 3000 mm and two available walls: A1, 6000 mm long, and A2, 4000 mm long. Canal Library has a clear interior of 6000 × 5000 × 2800 mm and wall B1, 4200 mm long. Both have a centred south entrance and a 1500 mm clear circulation route. The attached site records fix the wall anchors, loading doors and goods lifts. Develop the supplied kit into three freestanding frames, each 1200 × 1800 × 500 mm, distributed across A1 and A2 at Market Room and grouped along B1 at Canal Library. The kit travels in three crates, each 1360 × 1040 × 440 mm externally. Its aluminium members, plywood graphic carriers, hand-knob connections and component quantities are fixed in the site records.

Set photograph lighting to 50 lux and separately lit label areas to 150 lux. Label body text is at least 24 pt, with its centre 1100 mm above the floor; photograph centres are 1450 mm above the floor. Carry those settings into the installation drawings and visitor views. Each venue supplies two seats, with a 900 × 1400 mm clear wheelchair space beside one seat.

Audience: residents exploring changes along Cedar Street and community venue operators deciding how to host, install and move the exhibition.
