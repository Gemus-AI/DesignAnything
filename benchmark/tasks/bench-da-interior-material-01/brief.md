ROOM SWITCH | A compact rental room for work, meals and rest

Our rental room serves one person who works at home, eats at the same table and sleeps here. The internal envelope is 3.6 m east–west by 4.8 m north–south, with a 2.6 m ceiling. Coordinates start at the southwest internal corner: x runs east, y north and z upwards. The south doorway runs from x = 0.2 to 1.0 m, is 2.1 m high and has an inward-opening leaf hinged at its west jamb. The north window runs from x = 1.2 to 2.8 m, with a 0.9 m sill and a 1.2 m opening height. The radiator below it projects 0.15 m into the room; a 0.3 × 0.3 m full-height service shaft occupies the northeast corner. Retain the shell, openings, services, floor and ceiling light in their recorded positions. New additions are freestanding storage, textiles and plug-in lighting.

The retained furniture is one pine bed, 2000 × 1000 × 650 mm; one white table, 1100 × 600 × 740 mm; one beech chair with a green seat, 450 × 500 × 800 mm; and one cream sliding-door wardrobe, 800 × 550 × 1900 mm.
