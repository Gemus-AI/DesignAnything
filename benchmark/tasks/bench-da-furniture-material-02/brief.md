BETWEEN SHELVES | Reading seats and accessories for a retained library interior

Develop a coordinated furniture set around the retained bookcases of Thomas Hughes Public Library, Central Avenue, Rugby, Morgan County, Tennessee, using the attached archival geometry. The programme comprises two separate single-person reading seats S1 and S2, one two-person companion seat C1, one shared book support A1 and one shared bag rest A2: five physical units and four sitting places. Each place has a nominal clear sitting area 480 mm wide by 440 mm deep, at 450 mm above its local floor. A1 supports an open book 320 mm wide by 240 mm deep on a surface at 650 mm. A2 accommodates two upright bags simultaneously, each 300 mm wide by 180 mm deep by 400 mm high. Print "BETWEEN SHELVES" and "Four places, five units" on the furniture overview and presentation cover.

The bookcases anchor this project. Retain the drawn inner room boundary, openings, door swings, windows, built-in partitions and cupboards, every case footprint and every shelf tier, including both faces of projecting cases and the storage length and depth of wall cases. Reserve a continuous nominal 1200 mm public passage from the existing entrance approach into the central reading area, ending before the rear retrieval band, and nominal 900 mm retrieval bands at every accessible shelf face. Furniture, stored bags and occupied seating envelopes belong outside those bands and door swings. Preserve the entrance opening as drawn. All five units support themselves on the floor, with removable mechanical joints and protective feet. At least one accessory relates visibly to a retained case end and the new seating, with its separation dimensioned and the shelf faces accessible. At least one upholstered sitting-contact element is replaceable. Resolve the material allocation, connection hardware, tool access and maintenance in the opening specification, then carry those decisions through the drawings and use scenes.

Audience: library users needing short browsing, companion reading and waiting seats, together with the library team and furniture workshop deciding the arrangement and furniture development.
