BELL YARD VOICES | An oral history documentary opening

We are preparing a local history documentary about Bell Yard Hall. Design its 15-second opening as six shots, moving through 1938, 1964, 1991 and 2018 before a present-day illustration and the title. The four photographs establish the chronology; Ruth Cole's recorded memories carry the story. Print "BELL YARD VOICES" and "A street remembers" on the final title frame.

The archive contains photographs taken at Bell Yard Hall, Bell Yard, on 12 June 1938 by Arthur Bell, 8 September 1964 by Joan Mercer, 21 November 1991 by Peter Shaw and 14 April 2018 by Helen Cole. All four are cleared for film and promotion, with photographer credit required. The hall's five upper windows, central arched doorway and circular gable vent connect the decades. Ruth appears in the 2018 photograph; her short silver bob, round dark glasses and navy cardigan also identify her in the present-day drawing. The opening uses a 16:9 frame. The attached geometry also establishes a 9:16 frame for future adaptations, with action margins of 3.5% and graphics margins of 5% on every edge in both proportions.

Audience: local history documentary viewers, with the director and community history group using this work to approve the opening's chronology, portrayal and edit.
