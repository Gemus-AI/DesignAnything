HILLFOLD STAY | Six guest rooms along a village hillside

We are opening HILLFOLD STAY for visitors spending a few nights in Hillfold village. We need six double guest rooms for 12 guests, with a shared kitchen and dining room where our local reception team can host breakfast. The site is 36 m east to west by 30 m north to south. Ground rises from +0.000 m at the southwest corner to +3.000 m along the north boundary. A village path crosses the full site between y = 3.800 m and y = 6.200 m; its 2400 mm clear width and continuous public passage are fixed. Guest arrival, breakfast seating and luggage handling need their own space beside this everyday route.

Develop single-storey volumes on two terraces, with finished floors at +1.200 m and +2.400 m. Guest Rooms 01–03 belong on the Lower Terrace with Reception, Kitchen and Dining, Laundry and Plant; Guest Rooms 04–06 belong on the Upper Terrace. Each guest room has 24 m² net area including its bathroom. Kitchen and Dining has 36 m² and 12 seats; Reception has 12 m²; Laundry and Plant each have 6 m². Clear room height is 2.700 m. Building setbacks are 3 m from every site boundary, and the maximum roof ridge is +7.500 m. Use 30-degree clay-tile roofs and exposed glulam beams inside Kitchen and Dining, with principal beam spans up to 4.800 m. Develop the remaining material palette from the village's rubble-stone walls and recessed openings. Print "HILLFOLD STAY" on the settlement plan and presentation cover, and label the public route "Village path" on the settlement and Lower Terrace plans.

Audience: visiting guests and the local operating team, supporting the owner's layout decision and the architect's next design stage.
