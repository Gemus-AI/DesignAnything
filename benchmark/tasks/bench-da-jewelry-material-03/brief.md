VELT | Titanium housing and interchangeable finishes for a smart ring

We are developing VELT for adults who wear a health-tracking ring as everyday jewellery and want to change its appearance between work and evening occasions. Design a Grade 5 titanium housing and three interchangeable titanium caps for our existing M18 module. The finishes are Satin, with a brushed surface; Ink, with black PVD over a brushed surface; and Flute, with milled grooves and a satin surface. Each cap occupies the upper 120° of the ring and attaches to the same housing. The assembled ring, with any cap fitted, has a maximum outside diameter of 25 mm and a maximum axial width of 8.5 mm.

The black M18 module has an 18 mm clear bore, a 22 mm outside diameter and a 7 mm axial width; the intended finger circumference is 56–57 mm. Its inner sensor window measures 4 mm circumferentially by 5 mm axially. Two charging contacts, each 1 mm in diameter, sit on the front annular face. The attached drawing locates these features: angles run clockwise from the top in front view, and z runs from the front face towards the rear. The sensor centre is at 180°, z = 3.5 mm; the contact centres are at radius 10 mm, at 150° and 210°. Provide a 1.6 mm clear access opening over each contact and preserve the full sensor window and clear bore. Develop the cap attachment outside the module envelope, with an illustrated release action and rounded exposed edges. Print "VELT" and "One ring, three finishes" on the finish catalogue.

Audience: our jewellery lead, product team and manufacturing partner, choosing the housing and cap system for a sampling brief aimed at adult everyday wearers.
