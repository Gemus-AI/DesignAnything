SMALL WILD NEIGHBOURS | A children's field notebook for English town nature

We are developing an illustrated nature notebook for children aged 8–12 and their accompanying adults, for short daytime visits to English town parks and gardens from spring to early autumn. Print the title "SMALL WILD NEIGHBOURS", the subtitle "A nature notebook for English towns" and the age label "Ages 8–12" on the front cover. The six subjects are European robin, Blue tit, Common daisy, White clover, 7-spot ladybird and Garden snail. The shared page plan reserves pages 2–41; the samples listed below are supplied, and every index uses the same six overview page pairs.

The direction is original observational ink-and-colour-wash illustration, with visible ink strokes, translucent washes and recognisable natural colour patches and structures. Use three layout colours: dark green #24352A, pale green #DCE5C9 and rust #AD5B3C; organism colours follow the species facts and appearance photographs. Single pages measure 148 × 210 mm, with two text columns per page, a 4 mm column gap, 6 mm outer margins and a 6 mm text-and-feature safe band on each side of the binding fold. Each overview contains the exact common and scientific names, three short fact statements, at least two labels pointing to the illustrated features and one observation question. Use the method labels Pause, Notice and Record in that order. Recording pages use the exact labels I noticed, I wonder and Not seen; the index heading is "Find a neighbour". Observation takes place quietly with an adult, from an accessible path or permitted garden, with plants rooted and animals left where they are. Use the supplied practice record for the worked form, headed "Practice example — not a field record".

Audience: children and parents using the book outdoors, and our editorial and design teams deciding the page system and illustrated sample set for publication.
