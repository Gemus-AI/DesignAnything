CIRCA | A countertop food-scrap dryer for a compact kitchen

We are developing CIRCA for one-to-two-person households that need somewhere to hold and process daily preparation scraps on a small kitchen counter. Design the complete appliance: enclosure, loading lid, bucket grip, controls, service doors, feet and ventilation. Our process mixes and dries scraps in the removable B1 bucket, seated inside the heated C1 cradle and driven by D1 below it. Exhaust travels through F1; E1 controls the sequence. The nominal fill limit is 2.0 L, and the loading example is 1.0 L of drained vegetable peelings and cut fruit scraps. Heating and mixing begin with the bucket seated and lid closed; the lid remains locked through Running and Cooling, with normal opening and removal available in Ready.

The B1 body is diameter 180 × 170 H mm, within a diameter 190 × 180 H mm removal and stowed-grip envelope. Retain its circular rim, flat base, bottom coupling and attached mixing feature. Develop the whole machine within 300 W × 350 D × 360 H mm, including feet and closed lid, and state its actual dimensions. The kitchen bay is 600 W × 600 D mm, with the countertop 900 mm above the floor and the upper cabinet underside 620 mm above the counter. Resolve the opening lid, a 200 mm vertical bucket lift and a 140 mm straight filter withdrawal in that bay. Use a finely textured PC/ABS outer enclosure in #D8D1C5, a brushed 304 stainless steel bucket and a removable silicone lid gasket. Print the product name "CIRCA" on the appliance and "MAX 2.0 L" inside B1 below the rim. The interface wording is fixed to Start, Idle, Running, Cooling, Ready, Close lid and Install bucket; Unplugged identifies the maintenance condition.

Audience: our product team and appliance development partner, for selection of the complete product proposal and preparation of its mechanical development brief.
