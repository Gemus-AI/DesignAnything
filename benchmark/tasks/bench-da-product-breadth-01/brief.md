PALM LOOP | A desktop sampler for hands-on music making

We are commissioning a complete portable desktop music sampler for adult hobbyists who begin musical ideas by pressing pads and turning knobs. It records through LINE IN and plays four stored sounds. The enclosure silhouette, control arrangement and graphic identity are open. The finished instrument fits within 320 mm width, 220 mm depth and 85 mm overall height, including controls and feet. Its four pads each have a 36 × 36 mm exposed surface, with at least 8 mm between adjacent pad edges and at least 6 mm between a pad edge and any exposed knob or button edge. Use a fine-textured matte PC/ABS enclosure, silicone pads and satin anodised aluminium knob caps. Print "PALM LOOP" on the instrument's upper face.

The instrument uses the complete module set in sampler-inputs.en.md: four pads, four knobs, three buttons, three indicators, a processor, an internal battery and three connectors. Physical controls and indicator lights carry the complete interaction. Functional labels are P1, P2, P3, P4, VOLUME, TEMPO, START, LENGTH, SAMPLE, PLAY/STOP, POWER, REC, PLAY, USB-C POWER, LINE IN and HEADPHONES. Holding "SAMPLE" while pressing a pad selects the recording target; subsequent SAMPLE presses start and finish recording. "PLAY/STOP" starts and stops loop playback. The Operation board comprises four separately delivered panels: Idle, Armed, Recording and Playback. P2 is the target in Armed and Recording. The seated player uses a 720 mm high desk with a clear area of 500 × 350 mm. All three connectors occupy the edge farthest from the player, with the line-source and headphone cables routed behind the playing area. A1 remains internal during use and is accessible through a removable underside battery cover.

Audience: the product owner and instrument development team, for selecting the complete device proposal and briefing its first physical prototype.
