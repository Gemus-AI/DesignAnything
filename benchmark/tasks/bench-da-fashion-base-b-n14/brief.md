COMMON LOOP | Repairable knitwear for autumn and winter

We are developing COMMON LOOP for adults moving between home, work and everyday travel. The collection has three fixed styles: P01 Pullover in Rust #9B513D, C01 Cardigan in Navy #253544 and V01 Vest in Cream #E6DDCA. Develop these as a coordinated autumn and winter wardrobe, carrying forward the attached yarn colours and the relaxed proportions of our previous collection. The cardigan has five 18 mm corozo buttons. Our office combination is P01 under V01 under an open C01; the pullover neckline, vest neckline and cardigan opening must each remain visible. The fabric family is fixed: M01 is 80% wool, 20% nylon, 12-gauge double moss stitch, 380 g/m², with an 8 × 8 mm texture repeat and 1400 mm usable width; M02 is the same composition in 12-gauge 2 × 2 rib, 420 g/m², with 1000 mm usable width. Both have a washed, unbrushed finish. P01 and C01 have replaceable M02 cuffs, 60 mm deep, joined by removable hand linking. Their relaxed joining circumferences are 220 mm in S, 240 mm in M and 260 mm in L. Each sleeve carries an external M01 elbow patch, 80 × 100 mm, centred on the back sleeve line 180 mm above the cuff joining seam. P01 patches are Cream; C01 patches are Rust. Patch stitching runs vertically: 5 mm stitches, 5 mm gaps, rows 10 mm apart and a 5 mm edge inset. The attached size records fix the garment measurements. Each style carries a 75 × 20 mm inside back-neck label printed with "COMMON LOOP" and "Made to be mended" on separate lines. Detail the remaining neckline, hem and pocket choices as one coherent family.

Audience: our clothing design team, knitwear workshop and retail team, for the collection selection and sampling brief.
