MEMENTO | Heirloom carnelian pendant reset

I want one pendant that lets an inherited carved stone remain the focus of an everyday piece. G1 is the single reddish carnelian intaglio in stone-front.jpg, recorded by The Metropolitan Museum of Art as object 244973, accession 81.6.102, Gift of John Taylor Johnston, 1881. Its published overall dimensions are 1.3 × 0.9 × 0.4 cm; the design basis is a nominal 13 × 9 × 4 mm envelope. Preserve its irregular oval outline, existing small edge losses, translucent red body and incised figure/chariot/serpent scene. The carved face faces outward. engraving-impression.jpg supplies a grey, mirrored positive reading of that carving; stone-front.jpg supplies the gemstone’s appearance.

Develop a new setting, bail and coordinating chain in 925 silver, with polished, satin or oxidized finishes in locations you choose. G1 remains one intact stone with its existing surfaces and edge losses. Give it visible mechanical support and retention around the perimeter, with the central engraved scene readable. The pendant including its bail fits within 30 mm high × 24 mm wide × 10 mm deep. Choose one chain length from 420–500 mm and a generic silver clasp. The same adult wearer appears in W1, an open neckline with the pendant against the upper chest, and W2, a fine knit top with the pendant on the fabric. Print "MEMENTO" on all six boards and "925 silver" on the specification and stone-to-setting boards.

Audience: the holder choosing a daily-wear redesign and the jeweller reviewing its proportions and mounting arrangement.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the stone record, silver specification, dimensional limits and wearing contexts. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the six deliverables with their proportions, formats and stages. stone-front.jpg is the identity image for G1’s carved face, colour, outline and edge losses. engraving-impression.jpg is a reference for reading the carving through its mirrored positive impression. stone-envelope.png is the constraint drawing of the nominal stone envelope and pendant size limits. everyday-neckline.png is an atmosphere reference for the two clothing contexts and upper-torso framing.
