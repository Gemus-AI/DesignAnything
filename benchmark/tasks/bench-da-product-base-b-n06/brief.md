NESTPAIR | Independent water and slow-feeding products for compact homes

Develop NESTPAIR as two complete, independently usable products: W, a passive water vessel, and F, a slow feeder with a removable obstacle insert, joined when needed by detachable clip C. Each product has its own base and removable pad. The family direction is calm and domestic, with related rims, grips, seams and exterior finishes. Develop the body silhouettes, feeding path and release details around the supplied side interface.

Each product fits within 180 W x 180 D x 90 H mm; the joined pair fits within 362 W x 180 D x 90 H mm. W-R has a clear internal well 150 mm in diameter and 35 mm deep, with a nominal fill mark reading "350 mL" below the rim. F-R has a well 140 mm in diameter and 25 mm deep; removable F-I rises at most 18 mm above its floor and leaves food paths at least 12 mm wide. The complete service inventory is W-B, W-R, W-P, F-B, F-R, F-I, F-P and C. Both sides of each base carry a 36 x 20 mm connection patch with a 28 x 4 x 3 mm recess; C bridges a 2 mm gap. Start with W left and F right, then retain the same parts for reversal and separate use.

Print "NESTPAIR" in every board title and on the presentation cover, and "Detach C before lifting" on the assembly diagram and clip detail sheets. Propose specific material grades, finishes and contact zones for all eight parts. Bowl materials match each other, base finishes match each other, and the two removable elastomer pads sit beneath their respective bases.

Audience: households sharing compact homes with adult domestic cats, and our product and manufacturing teams deciding the range and preparing its development brief.
