Fit out SECOND SEAM around browsing, fitting, handover, repair and collection. Lighting has three separately switched layers: L1 ceiling linear ambient lights at 3000 K over sales and circulation, L2 articulated task lights at 4000 K at each sewing and pressing table, and L3 adjustable track spotlights at 3000 K over the four sale rails. Place the three switches on the staff side of the counter.
