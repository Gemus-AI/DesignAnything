SMALL HOURS | Adult embroidery starter kit product visuals

We sell embroidery kits to adults starting their first handwork project. Build the product visuals for our own online shop around a warm white background, pine green #264C43 headings and cream #F5F0E6 information panels. Print "SMALL HOURS" and "Your first stitches, clearly guided" on the main images and vertical shopping cards. Our two individually sold kits are Fern Study, EK-01, USD 24, and Daisy Study, EK-02, USD 24. Fern Study has one stem with 12 leaves in six pairs; Daisy Study has three flowers with eight petals each. Each motif occupies 100 × 100 mm.

Each kit contains one wooden hoop with a 150 mm inner diameter and 166 mm outer diameter, one 250 × 250 mm preprinted cotton square, four 8 m skeins of six-strand cotton, two No. 7 embroidery needles measuring 38 mm, one 100 × 140 mm stitch guide and one 270 × 270 × 35 mm kraft box. Both kits use T01 Pine #365744, T02 Sage #90A889, T03 Cream #F3E9D2 and T04 Ochre #C99236. The guide follows four stages: mount the fabric, separate two strands and thread the needle, stitch the motif, finish the back. Label the supplied materials "In your kit", scissors "You supply: scissors", and completed work "Finished example - stitch it yourself". The finished embroidery is the project outcome.
