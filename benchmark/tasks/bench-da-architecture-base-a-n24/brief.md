RIVER COMMONS | Waterfront public library and summer refuge

We are commissioning a public library and summer refuge within boundary A–D on the attached waterfront site. The design area is approximately 4,563 m², with recorded boundary lengths of approximately 24 / 147 / 39 / 145 m. The existing levee, railway and river edge establish the setting. Give residents a legible route between the street, library and waterfront, with separate access for deliveries and staff.

The building has an open ground level and two enclosed public floors of 3,400 m² each, totaling 6,800 m². Use the local datum ±0.000 recorded in source-status.md: the riverbank sample is −1.10 m, the railway embankment sample is +4.48 m and the project flood scenario is +3.5 m. Ground-level overhead clearance is 4.5 m; occupied floor levels are +4.80 m and +9.30 m, with the roof at +13.80 m. Provide a continuous 4 m clear waterfront route and step-free access to both occupied floors. The room schedule in 01-client-facts.en.md allocates 2,800 m² net accommodation to each floor. Use concrete supports, cross-laminated timber floors and exposed glulam beams in the Cooling Commons and Reading Room.

Summer refuge operates from June 1 to October 15, daily from 10:00 to 18:00, with 80 seats in the Cooling Commons. The Community Room accommodates 80 people from 18:00 to 21:00 through its own entrance and toilet access while the reading areas close. Print "RIVER COMMONS" at the public entrance and on drawing titles; print "Cooling Commons" on that room's entrance sign and plan label.

Audience: surrounding residents, library staff and the municipal project team, for choosing the building arrangement and agreeing how reading, summer refuge and evening activities share the site.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the dimensions, room areas, operating hours and material requirements. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md lists the deliverables, proportions, formats and stages. site-overhead.png is the identity source for the existing waterfront setting. site-plan.png is the constraint image for boundary A–D, north, scale and surrounding connections. source-status.md records the aerial's date and source, the vertical datum, sampled levels and the project flood scenario.
