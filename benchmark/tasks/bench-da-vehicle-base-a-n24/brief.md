LATER | An electric two-wheeler for the journey home at night

We are developing LATER, a single-adult seated electric two-wheeler for short journeys home through dense urban neighbourhoods. Give the complete vehicle a contemporary, purposeful form, with the rear compartment and two substantial main side covers integrated into its body. Print "LATER" on both main side covers. The platform has exactly two inline wheels, a 1180 mm wheelbase, tyres of 450 mm outer diameter and 90 mm width, and a rear hub-drive envelope of 180 mm diameter. The fixed battery B1 and controller C1 positions are recorded in the attached facts and hard-point drawing. The complete closed vehicle, including unfolded mirrors and its deployed stand, fits within X -250–1500, Y -350–350 and Z 0–1250 mm. Retain the seat reference at (450,0,750), grip centres at (970,±300,980), the clear 260 × 240 mm footboard at Z 240 and at least 150 mm body-underfloor clearance. Use a steel support structure, painted ABS main covers and rear shell, a coated-polyester seat, rubber footboard and polypropylene storage divider; select their finishes.

The rear compartment carries A1, a rigid 340 × 250 × 30 mm laptop sleeve, and B2, an 80 mm diameter × 260 mm bottle, together behind the seat. Both long axes follow X; the sleeve lies flat and the bottle lies horizontally. Each retained zone provides at least 5 mm side/end clearance and 5 mm overhead. Either item is reachable while the other stays in place. The left main cover opens or detaches for inspection; print "POWER OFF - LEFT COVER ACCESS" on its access illustration. Develop both Ink and Pearl on the same geometry. Ink carries the night and main parking work. Provide one white headlamp, one red tail/brake assembly, four amber indicator units and one passive recognition strip on each side, with OFF, NIGHT and BRAKE examples. P1 is an 1800 × 800 mm bay with a rear wall, an unavailable neighbouring side and a 900 mm left access aisle. The upright vehicle parks straight with mirrors unfolded and at least 25 mm to every bay edge; the storage lid and moving cover remain inside the bay throughout access.

Audience: our vehicle design and product teams, deciding the whole-vehicle proposal for urban commuters who return after dark, carry everyday belongings and park in crowded residential areas.
