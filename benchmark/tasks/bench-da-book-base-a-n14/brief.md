THE ENGRAVED SKY | Reader of nineteen historical astronomy plates

Meridian Sheet Press is re-editing nineteen public-domain astronomy plates from Wellcome Collection into a reader that an adult can sit and read through and also look things up in. Nineteen plates, five chapters, names fixed: 1 Coloured Constellation Cards (nine plates), 2 Charts by Right Ascension (two plates), 3 Whole-Sky and Cosmography (three plates), 4 Instruments (two plates), 5 Comets and Observation (three plates). The plates argue with each other: plate-03 is a small hand-coloured card, plate-14 is a whole sheet of engraved panel lettering, plate-18 arrives with two columns of its own printed caption text, and plate-01 and plate-10 are the same constellation drawn twice, once as a coloured card and once as a line projection corrected to 1808. One grid has to hold all of them.

The book is 240 × 300 mm portrait, 128 pages, 150 gsm matt coated text stock, section-sewn paperback, spine 12 mm, printed four-colour in a run of 600. Primary ink #1B2A4A. The page grid is six columns of 29.5 mm with 5 mm gutters, inner margin 20 mm, outer 18 mm, head 20 mm, foot 24 mm, and the caption of every plate spread sits in the lowest 34 mm of the type area. Each plate caption carries four fields in this order: the collection title as recorded, the accession number, the catalogue date and maker line, and the credit. Credit the source as "Wellcome Collection" and give the rights label Public Domain Mark. Where the catalogue records no year, print "No date recorded"; where it records no maker, print "No maker recorded". The front cover carries the title THE ENGRAVED SKY and the descriptor "Nineteen Plates of the Historical Sky", the spine reads top to bottom, the imprint is Meridian Sheet Press, and the ISBN 978-1-7396-4412-8 barcode sits in the lower right of the back cover at 40 × 25 mm.

Audience: the imprint's editor and production manager, who choose which layout goes to press and hand the pages to the printer.
