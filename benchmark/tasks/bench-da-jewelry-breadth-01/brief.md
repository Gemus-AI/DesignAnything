ARCLET | An adjustable ear clip and cuff with a detachable chain

We are developing an ear jewellery system for adults who express their style through non-pierced accessories. Design exactly one left-ear lobe clip C, one left-ear helix cuff H and one detachable chain K. Our two fitting profiles are E1, an attached lobe with ear height 60 mm, maximum width 30 mm, lobe height 16 mm and lobe thickness 5 mm; and E2, a free lobe with corresponding dimensions of 70 mm, 35 mm, 20 mm and 7 mm. C contacts only the 10 mm region L at the lower lobe edge; H contacts only the 12 mm region H along the upper outer rim. Both fixings use adjustable mechanical closures with visibly padded opposed contacts, leaving the ear canal open. The same decorative parts serve both profiles through adjustment.

Use uncoated titanium for the structural metal and chain, silicone contact pads, and optional translucent acrylic accents. C’s decorative body fits within 20 × 20 × 8 mm; H’s fits within 25 × 15 × 8 mm, with pads and chain outside those envelopes. Develop the chain construction and closures, with tool-free detachment at both ends and independent use of C and H. Choose an adjustable chain-length range within 65–95 mm. In use, K hangs with visible slack and its lowest point sits at most 30 mm below the lobe bottom. Use our existing twin-arc emblem and print "ARCLET" on all six boards; print "LEFT" on both wearing boards.

Audience: the product team and jewellery workshop, for selecting the finished design and planning its first prototypes for adult wearers without ear piercings.

3. One final materials board identifying titanium finishes and thicknesses, silicone pad forms and dimensions, any acrylic accent colour, and prototype priorities for fit, skin contact, edge finish and retention
4. One standalone product board with front and rear views of C and H in their independently wearable states and K laid out separately with both end closures open
5. One E1 wearing board showing the complete linked system on the attached-lobe left ear, the full ear contour, a rear/contact inset, selected chain length and vertical lobe-bottom offset
6. One E2 wearing board showing the same linked system on the free-lobe left ear, the full ear contour, a rear/contact inset, selected chain length and vertical lobe-bottom offset
