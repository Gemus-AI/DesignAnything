FOLD FIELD | A reconfigurable textile screen for shared homes

We are commissioning a freestanding textile screen for adults who share a living room and need a video-call background or a place to sit alone for a while. The room measures 4200 × 3600 mm, with a ceiling height of 2500 mm. The attached plan fixes the entrance, window, desk, sofa and storage position. The complete screen must fit within a storage envelope 700 mm wide × 400 mm deep × 1850 mm high and leave a clear route at least 900 mm wide from the entrance to the desk in both occupied configurations.

Develop three connected frames, each 600 mm wide × 1800 mm high, using solid ash with clear water-based lacquer. Frame sections, supports, connectors and textile attachments are yours to resolve; assembly and changes between extended, corner and stored states must be tool-free. Our existing fabrics are A, an opaque oat-colored weave of 70% cotton and 30% linen, 320 g/m², supplied at 1400 mm width; and B, a translucent rust-colored weave of 100% polyester, 120 g/m², supplied at 1500 mm width. Both are machine washable at 30°C. Use both fabrics, with removable panels, and carry the same frame, support, connector and textile inventory through all three states. Any loose parts belong within the storage envelope. Material expression should come from the contrast between opaque and translucent cloth, its tension, edges and overlaps. Print "FOLD FIELD" on all six sheets and "Room to yourself" as the caption on the domestic scene sheet.

Audience: our furniture development team and sample workshop, for choosing the screen configuration and preparing the first prototype.

Deliverables:

2. One extended-state sheet with a room plan, perspective and overall dimensions, showing the screen behind the desk user and the 900 mm clear route
3. One corner-state sheet with a room plan, perspective and overall dimensions, showing a seated retreat, a 90-degree connection and the 900 mm clear route
4. One stored-state sheet with front, side and plan views, overall dimensions and the locations of all three frames, textiles, supports and connectors within the storage envelope
5. One textile construction sheet with the A and B panel arrangement, both fabric records, edge and overlap details, attachment close-ups and the removal sequence for washing
6. One domestic scene sheet containing two views of the same design: an extended video-call background and a corner reading retreat
