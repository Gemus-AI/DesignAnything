LUMA | AI glasses and a charging case for everyday transitions

We are developing LUMA for adults who want to capture a moment during an urban pause and move comfortably between work and leisure. The system comprises one complete pair of AI glasses and one matching charging/storage case. Connected assistance uses an intentionally captured image and a short, user-initiated spoken question through a paired phone. The glasses themselves are the main design task: two clear non-display lenses, a complete frame, folding temples, integrated electronics, comfortable contact surfaces and reachable physical controls. Print "LUMA" on the glasses and case.

The glasses mass target is at most 42 g. Choose a front width of 140–150 mm and a hinge-axis-to-tip centreline length of 135–150 mm for each temple; the largest temple section is at most 18 mm high and 12 mm thick. Folded glasses fit within 150 × 60 × 48 mm; the closed case fits within 170 × 85 × 65 mm, with at least 2 mm cavity clearance on each opposing side of the chosen folded envelope. Use the eleven recorded electronic modules, with 1 mm between envelopes and 1 mm enclosure outside them except at functional openings. The right-temple underside carries two flush pads of diameter 2 mm, spaced 6 mm centre to centre, both within 60 mm of the hinge; P+ is nearer the hinge. Matching case tips have diameter 1.2 mm. Use satin TR90 for the frame and temple shells, anodised aluminium hinges and silicone nose and ear contact surfaces. Develop the colour palette. Make B01 accessible for user replacement and print "User-replaceable B01" beside its removal detail.

The three glasses states are READY, CAPTURE and PRIVACY. READY exposes the camera with its indicator off; CAPTURE exposes the camera with a steady white front indicator; PRIVACY closes an opaque slider over the camera and disables camera and microphone capture. Give the slider a visible position mark. Folded glasses dock with the slider closed; the case indicator is steady amber during charging and off during stored/idle. Its indicator remains visible with the lid closed. Label these states on their corresponding design boards.

Audience: the product owner, industrial design team and engineering team, choosing the complete eyewear concept and preparing its first development brief.

Deliverables:
1. One complete glasses anatomy board with front, top and right orthographic views, chosen open and folded dimensions, all eight glasses modules, schematic wiring, material callouts and an exploded B01 replacement detail
2. One READY wearing board for W1 everyday, with a three-quarter upper-body view and nose/ear contact inset
3. One CAPTURE operation board with the reachable button, camera opening and illuminated front indicator
4. One PRIVACY wearing and operation board for W2 work pause, with the closed slider, its position mark and a hand near the capture button
5. One open-case charging board with the folded glasses seated, a mating-contact section, all three case modules, cavity dimensions, frame supports and removal access
6. One closed-case stored/idle board with an empty open-case inset, the external indicator, chosen outer dimensions and coordinated material and colour callouts

Acceptance:
- On the anatomy board, dimension the front width within 140–150 mm, both temple lengths within 135–150 mm, temple sections within 18 × 12 mm and the actual folded envelope within 150 × 60 × 48 mm; label C01, B01, B02, S01, S02, K01, M01 and I01 with their recorded dimensions and local-axis orientation, retaining 1 mm envelope separation and enclosure.
- On the anatomy and CAPTURE boards, show K01's 4 mm optical aperture, I01 within 12 mm of its centre, and a capture-button reach that leaves the aperture and lenses clear; on the anatomy board, identify satin TR90, anodised aluminium and silicone, and show B01's access cover, detachable connection and removal arrow beside User-replaceable B01.
- On the W1 READY, CAPTURE and W2 PRIVACY boards, print the corresponding state labels and W codes where assigned; show respectively an exposed camera with its light off, an exposed camera with a steady white light, and a fully covered camera with its light off, a physical position mark and camera/microphone capture disabled when the button is pressed.
- On the charging board, label both chosen pad-centre distances within 60 mm of the right hinge, 2 mm pad diameter, 6 mm centre spacing and 1.2 mm case-tip diameter; align P+ and P- with their matching tips, show parallel contact faces and a locating key, and seat the fully folded glasses with the slider closed and the case indicator steady amber.
- On the charging and stored/idle boards, repeat the anatomy board's folded dimensions, dimension at least 2 mm cavity clearance on all six sides except marked support/contact interfaces, label CB01, CC01 and U01 with their recorded dimensions, and keep the closed case within 170 × 85 × 65 mm; show lens-clear supports, removal access, the empty cavity and the closed-lid indicator switched off in stored/idle.
- Across all six boards, preserve the same frame, module positions, openings, seams, hinge construction and palette; show LUMA on the glasses in the anatomy board and on the case in the stored/idle board.
