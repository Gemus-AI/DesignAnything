COMMON YARD | Private rooms and shared living for young city residents

We are developing COMMON YARD for young adults who want manageable housing costs and a private room to return to. The building will house 18 residents in 18 single-occupancy rooms, each with its own bathroom: 16 rooms of 20 m² and two accessible rooms of 26 m². There are three occupied floors, with six rooms on each floor; both accessible rooms are on the ground floor. Establish the accommodation schedule first, then develop the building around it. The arrangement of rooms, shared spaces and courtyard is open.

The level site measures 30000 mm east–west by 36000 mm north–south, with the street on the south. Setbacks are 3000 mm east and west and 4000 mm north and south. Ground floor ±0.000 coincides with the south pavement; upper floors are +3.200 m and +6.400 m, the roof datum is +9.600 m and the height limit is +10.200 m. Enclosed rooms have 2800 mm clear height. Structural spans are at most 6000 mm. Two stairs and one lift occupy the western 6000 mm strip of the buildable envelope, with one stair at each end. Neighbouring buildings stand 6000 mm beyond the east and west boundaries and 8000 mm beyond the north boundary. The existing east neighbour has three storeys, a height of 9600 mm and four window bays on its site-facing elevation. Our material palette is a cast-in-place reinforced concrete frame, brick infill and aluminium window frames; concrete soffits remain exposed in Shared Kitchen, Dining Room and Lounge. All shared facilities are on the ground floor: Shared Kitchen, 24 m² with six cooking positions and 6000 mm of usable worktop; Dining Room, 36 m² with 18 seats; Lounge, 30 m² with 12 seats; Work Room, 18 m² with six desks; Laundry, 12 m² with three washers and three dryers; Entrance Lobby, 12 m²; Guest WC, 6 m²; and Service Room, 12 m². An open courtyard provides at least 60 m². Print "COMMON YARD" at the entrance and "Resident access" at the controlled residential thresholds.

Audience: the housing operator and prospective young residents, deciding whether the proposal offers affordable shared living with clear private boundaries.
