NORTHBOUND | Regional frozen-food delivery packaging

We need secondary delivery packaging for customers ordering Otaru Double Cheese away from its home region. Our service identity is "NORTHBOUND", with the descriptor "Regional frozen-food delivery". U01 is one complete sealed retail and foam package containing one whole cake of listed diameter 120 mm; its bounding envelope is 160 W × 163 D × 94 H mm. K1 SINGLE, order NB-401, carries one U01. K2 DOUBLE, order NB-402, carries two identical U01 units side by side, upright. The existing retail packages remain intact throughout packing and removal. Our controlled frozen distribution service handles storage and transport; the packaging information should lead recipients clearly from receiving to frozen storage or preparation for serving.

The carton geometry is fixed. K1 has an external envelope of 196 × 200 × 124 mm, a clear interior of 188 × 192 × 116 mm and a usable insert volume of 184 × 188 × 112 mm. K2 has corresponding dimensions of 372 × 200 × 124 mm, 364 × 192 × 116 mm and 360 × 188 × 112 mm. All dimensions follow W × D × H. Both cartons open at the front and hinge across the rear width. Use corrugated kraft paperboard for the cartons and uncoated paperboard for the retaining inserts. Develop the retention, lifting access and graphic system around these envelopes. Each carton needs a blank 100 × 70 mm carrier-label area on top, a separate 100 × 60 mm dispatch-label area on the front and graphics at least 8 mm from the hinge and closure. One 148 × 105 mm opening card sits in a lid pocket clear of the lifting path. Print "Keep frozen" on both cartons and dispatch labels; identify the contents as 1 whole cake on K1 and 2 whole cakes on K2. Use Otaru Double Cheese in plain type as the product identifier.

Audience: customers receiving regional food away from home, and our packing team, for correct order identification, packing and opening.
