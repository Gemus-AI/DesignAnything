ROUNDTRIP | Reusable apparel delivery and return packaging

We need a reusable packaging system for our online apparel orders and customer returns. ROUNDTRIP is the programme name; the wordmark, graphics, materials and structures are open for development. The family comprises S and M bags and an L box. Their maximum closed external dimensions are respectively 330 x 250 x 50 mm, 430 x 330 x 90 mm and 380 x 260 x 165 mm. Bag dimensions follow width x height x depth; box dimensions follow width x depth x height. Empty mass targets are 90 g, 160 g and 420 g or less. Material and closure choices should support a target of at least 20 cycles, each comprising an outbound journey and a return journey.

Every unit needs clearly distinguishable "OUTBOUND" and "RETURN" states, a separate 100 x 150 mm shipping-label zone for each journey, and tool-free reusable closures with replaceable tamper-evident indicators at SEAL 1 and SEAL 2. The customer covers or cleanly removes the outbound label and reveals or attaches the return label supplied inside. Permanent features are a 60 x 60 mm scan zone, an adjacent 60 x 10 mm identifier line and a CYCLE tally of 20 cells, each at least 8 x 8 mm. The scan zone and identifier line remain empty and are annotated on technical drawings. Every unit carries ROUNDTRIP and "REUSABLE MAILER · RETURN ME". Folded storage limits are 330 x 125 x 15 mm for S, 430 x 165 x 25 mm for M and 380 x 260 x 40 mm for L; ten folded M units fit within 430 x 165 x 250 mm.

Audience: customers receiving and returning apparel, warehouse teams handling both journeys, and couriers identifying the active label and scanning the permanent tracking area, with the work supporting our packaging selection and warehouse rollout planning.
