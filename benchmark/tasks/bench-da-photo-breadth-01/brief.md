ALDER COMMON LIBRARY | A morning journey from street to reading table

We are commissioning a morning architectural photography series for our community library's resident newsletter and an architectural journal feature. The sequence should let someone approaching for the first time understand how the street leads through the entrance to a place to read. Deliver five finished photographs and a final sequence sheet, with the exterior supplied in two crops from the same exposure.

The library is a single-storey rectangle, 18000 mm east to west and 12000 mm north to south, with a flat parapet at 3600 mm. A centred, 1800 mm-wide glazed double entrance occupies the south facade; the existing sign above it reads "ALDER COMMON LIBRARY". The east facade has three equal windows, each 2400 mm wide and 2100 mm high, with sills at 600 mm. Warm grey brick, charcoal aluminium frames, pale terrazzo flooring and one oak reading table establish the material continuity. The table stands beside the middle east window. The south entrance opens directly into the reading room through a 2400 mm-deep arrival zone.

The session uses this single building between 08:00 and 09:00. The supplied light condition is east-southeast sunlight at azimuth 105 degrees and altitude 20 degrees, casting shadows toward west-northwest. Use daylight as the sole illumination and a common 5500 K white balance. Camera positions and focal lengths are yours to choose within the mapped access areas. Print "A place to begin the day" as the heading of the final sequence sheet.

Audience: local residents deciding how the library fits into their morning routine, and architectural journal readers assessing its arrival experience, spatial connections and daylight.

Inputs (attached): 01-client-facts.en.md contains the building dimensions, opening locations, material records and morning light conditions. 02-delivery-catalog.en.md contains the proportions, formats and stages for all six deliverables. library-plan-and-crops.png supplies the measured plan, accessible camera areas, north orientation, light direction and publication crop geometry. library-facades-and-materials.png records the existing facades, entrance sign, window arrangement and material appearance. morning-light-study.png supplies an empty-space reference for low sunlight, shadow edges and surface texture.

Deliverables:
1. Two exterior arrival photographs from one exposure: a landscape view connecting the south approach, entrance and east facade, and a portrait crop retaining the entrance sign and nearest east window
2. One interior threshold photograph connecting the south entrance, arrival zone and east-side reading table
3. One reading-room photograph showing the oak table and all three east windows in their spatial relationship
4. One square material detail photograph of an east-window reveal, charcoal frame and sunlit terrazzo floor
5. One two-page final sequence and shooting-notes document, with all five photographs as numbered thumbnails, the four distinct camera positions marked on the plan, captions and exposure records
