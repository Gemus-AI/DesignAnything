KINVA | A working home robot demonstration booth

We are taking our K1 home service robot to a trade show and need a booth where visitors can watch a complete demonstration, inspect a second unit and sit down with our channel team. Our identity is fixed: print "KINVA" on the main fascia and product information panels, use primary colour #245C53, and print "See everyday assistance in action" on the fascia. Preserve the robot's warm grey body, dark sensor band, green chest insert and single right arm throughout the work.

The booth measures 10000 × 8000 mm, with a 3500 mm height limit, a closed north edge and 3000 mm aisles along its other three edges. The attached boundary drawing fixes the south entrance, east exit, western demonstration envelope and observation band. Provide 1500 mm clear visitor routes, an internal queue for four people, observation positions for six people including one wheelchair position, a four-seat meeting area and 6 m² of enclosed storage. Our three physical exhibits are HB-01, the working K1; HB-02, the stationary K1; and HB-03, its charging dock. Each robot measures 600 × 650 × 1150 mm; the dock measures 700 × 450 × 180 mm, all width × depth × height. Demonstrations run every 10 minutes: a 240-second sequence covers introduction, a 1500 mm drive to the shelf, transfer of one empty cup, return to the dock and reset. The booth's volume, furniture arrangement and graphic composition are open within these conditions.

Audience: trade show visitors learning how K1 is used, channel partners considering distribution, and demonstration staff preparing the live presentation.
