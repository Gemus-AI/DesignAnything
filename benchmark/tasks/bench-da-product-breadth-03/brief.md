WAYFARER | An everyday folding rollator for walking, resting and home storage

We are developing WAYFARER for older adults who walk around their neighbourhood, pause for a seated rest and store their rollator at home. Develop the complete four-wheel product: frame, adjustable grips, independent mechanical brakes, seat and back contact, attached bag, folding joints and retention. Our component and interaction rules are fixed; the frame layout, fork shapes, folding arrangement and control forms are yours to resolve. Give it the familiarity of an everyday household object, using 6061 aluminium tubing with a fine-textured powder coat and TPE grip contact surfaces. Establish the remaining materials and finishes in the component and state register.

The three grip settings are 800, 850 and 900 mm. Seat height is 460 mm, depth 320 mm and usable width at least 440 mm. Four attached wheels have tyres of diameter 200 mm and width 32 mm. The complete open product fits within 650 W × 750 D × 900 H mm; folded at the 800 mm grip setting, it fits within 300 W × 750 D × 900 H mm. Walking, stationary rest and storage use the same frame and attached components. Each hand control operates its corresponding rear brake. A seated rest uses the engaged open-frame lock and both parking brakes. Print "WAYFARER" on the frame, the complete-product views and the presentation cover. The brake board includes "Not parked: apply both brakes" beside the left-only parking example. Use the other state labels listed below exactly.

Audience: older adults and companions, our product team and the development workshop, for the concept selection and detailed development brief.
